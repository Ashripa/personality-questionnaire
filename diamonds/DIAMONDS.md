# DIAMONDS: Situational Eight DIAMONDS Taxonomy

## Overview

The DIAMONDS taxonomy describes eight major psychological characteristics of situations. Two measurement instruments are covered here:

1. **S8\*** (24 items) -- the optimized self-report measure of the Situational Eight DIAMONDS
2. **S8-I / S8-II** (8 items each) -- ultra-brief one-item-per-dimension versions

## DIAMONDS Dimensions

| Acronym Letter | Dimension   | Core Meaning |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| **D** | Duty        | A job/task has to be done; work is the focus |
| **I** | Intellect   | Deep thinking is possible or required |
| **A** | Adversity   | Someone is being threatened, blamed, or criticized |
| **M** | Mating      | Potential romantic/sexual partners or cues are present |
| **O** | pOsitivity  | The situation is enjoyable, pleasant, or simple |
| **N** | Negativity  | The situation is stressful, frustrating, or anxiety-inducing |
| **D** | Deception   | Someone could be deceived or is being untrustworthy |
| **S** | Sociality   | Social interaction, communication, or relationships are central |

---

## Instructions (English)

> Please think about the situation you just experienced (or: a typical situation in your daily life). For each of the following statements, please indicate **how characteristic** the statement is of that situation.

Response Scale: **1** = *not at all* to **7** = *totally*

## Reverse-Scoring

There are **no reverse-scored items** in the S8\* or the ultra-brief versions. All items are scored in the keyed direction.

---

## Scoring Method

1. **Subscale scores:** Compute the mean (or sum) of items belonging to each DIAMONDS dimension.
   - For the S8\* (24 items): 3--4 items per dimension.
   - For the S8-I / S8-II (8 items): 1 item per dimension (no aggregation within dimensions).
2. **Profile:** The eight dimension scores form a situational profile that can be compared across situations, persons, or contexts.
3. There is **no total score**; the DIAMONDS taxonomy is used as a profile measure.

## Psychometric Properties

### S8\* (24-item version)

| Property | Values |
|:--|:--|
| Internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) | .61 -- .90 across the 8 dimensions |
| Factor loadings | .57 -- .92 |
| Convergent validity with RSQ-8 | Supported; nomological networks replicate across measures |
| Sample sizes for validation | Up to N = 7,835 (sports context) |

### S8-I / S8-II (ultra-brief 8-item versions)

| Property | Values |
|:--|:--|
| Convergent validity with S8\* | Moderate to high correlations with corresponding S8\* subscales |
| Discriminant validity | Low cross-dimension correlations |
| Validated against | Four ultra-brief scales (S8-I, S8-II, S8-III-A, S8-III-P) were cross-validated using MTMM analyses |
