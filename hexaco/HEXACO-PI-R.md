## Overview

The HEXACO-PI-R (HEXACO Personality Inventory - Revised) is a self-report personality inventory developed by Kibeom Lee, Ph.D. and Michael C. Ashton, Ph.D. It measures six major dimensions of personality based on lexical studies of personality structure across multiple languages.

### Available Versions

| Version | Items | Items per Facet | Items per Domain | Admin Time | Recommended Use |
|---------|-------|-----------------|------------------|------------|-----------------|
| HEXACO-200 | 200 | 8 | 32 (+8 Altruism) | ~40 min | Highest facet-level reliability |
| HEXACO-100 | 100 | 4 | 16 (+4 Altruism) | ~20 min | Most research (recommended) |
| HEXACO-60 | 60 | 2-3 | 10 | ~12 min | Time-constrained studies |

All versions are available free for non-profit academic research at https://hexaco.org. Both self-report and observer-report forms are provided. Over 40 language translations are available.

## Structure: 6 Domains x 4 Facets = 24 Facets (+1 Interstitial)

### H - Honesty-Humility (诚实-谦逊)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Sincerity** (真诚) | Genuine vs. manipulative in interpersonal relations; high scorers avoid flattery and manipulation |
| **Fairness** (公平) | Avoidance of fraud and corruption; high scorers reject cheating and rule-breaking for personal gain |
| **Greed Avoidance** (不贪婪) | Disinterest in lavish wealth and luxury; high scorers are unmotivated by monetary or material gain |
| **Modesty** (谦虚) | Tendency toward humility; high scorers see themselves as ordinary, low scorers claim superiority |

### E - Emotionality (情绪性)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Fearfulness** (恐惧) | Tendency to experience fear of physical danger; high scorers avoid harm, low scorers are fearless |
| **Anxiety** (焦虑) | Tendency to worry; high scorers are preoccupied with problems, low scorers feel little stress |
| **Dependence** (依赖) | Need for emotional support from others; high scorers seek encouragement, low scorers are self-assured |
| **Sentimentality** (感伤) | Emotional bonds with others; high scorers feel empathy and attachment, low scorers are detached |

### X - eXtraversion (外向性)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Social Self-Esteem** (社会自尊) | Positive self-regard in social contexts; high scorers feel likable, low scorers feel worthless |
| **Social Boldness** (社交大胆) | Confidence in social situations; high scorers speak up publicly, low scorers feel shy |
| **Sociability** (社交性) | Enjoyment of social interaction; high scorers seek conversation and gatherings, low scorers prefer solitude |
| **Liveliness** (活力) | Enthusiasm and energy; high scorers are optimistic and cheerful, low scorers lack dynamism |

### A - Agreeableness (宜人性)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Forgivingness** (宽恕) | Willingness to trust after betrayal; high scorers re-establish relations, low scorers hold grudges |
| **Gentleness** (温和) | Mild treatment of others; high scorers are lenient in judging, low scorers are critical and harsh |
| **Flexibility** (灵活性) | Willingness to compromise; high scorers accommodate others, low scorers are stubborn |
| **Patience** (耐心) | Tendency to remain calm; high scorers control anger, low scorers lose temper quickly |

### C - Conscientiousness (尽责性)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Organization** (条理性) | Seeking order in surroundings; high scorers are tidy and structured, low scorers are sloppy |
| **Diligence** (勤勉) | Work ethic and self-discipline; high scorers push themselves to achieve goals, low scorers are unmotivated |
| **Perfectionism** (完美主义) | Attention to detail and accuracy; high scorers are thorough, low scorers tolerate errors |
| **Prudence** (审慎) | Careful deliberation before action; high scorers are cautious, low scorers act impulsively |

### O - Openness to Experience (开放性)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Aesthetic Appreciation** (审美鉴赏) | Enjoyment of art and nature; high scorers are absorbed by beauty, low scorers are unimpressed |
| **Inquisitiveness** (好奇心) | Desire for knowledge and learning; high scorers are curious, low scorers are incurious |
| **Creativity** (创造力) | Preference for innovation; high scorers seek novel solutions, low scorers avoid originality |
| **Unconventionality** (非传统性) | Acceptance of unusual ideas; high scorers embrace radical thinking, low scorers are conformist |

### Interstitial Scale (200-item and 100-item versions only)
| Facet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Altruism** (利他) | Sympathy and generosity toward others; high scorers are soft-hearted, low scorers are hard-hearted |

## Instructions (English)

> On the following pages you will find a series of statements about you. Please read each statement and decide how much you agree or disagree with that statement. Then write your response in the space next to the statement using the following scale:
>
> 5 = strongly agree
> 4 = agree
> 3 = neutral (neither agree nor disagree)
> 2 = disagree
> 1 = strongly disagree
>
> Please answer every statement, even if you are not completely sure of your response.

## Instructions (Chinese / 中文指导语)

> 下列的描述句是一些关于人们在日常生活中所表现出的行为或想法。填答时，请您仔细阅读每个描述句，然后在题号后的空格内填写一个数字，以代表您对该描述句的同意或不同意的程度。
>
> 5 = 非常同意
> 4 = 同意
> 3 = 没有意见（既不同意也不反对）
> 2 = 不同意
> 1 = 非常不同意
>
> 每题都请作答，切勿省略。

## Version Comparison: Item Structure

### HEXACO-200 (Full Version)
- 6 domains x 4 facets x 8 items = 192 items
- 1 interstitial facet (Altruism) x 8 items = 8 items
- Total: 200 items
- Provides the highest facet-level reliability
- Available upon request from the authors

### HEXACO-100 (Standard Version)
- 6 domains x 4 facets x 4 items = 96 items
- 1 interstitial facet (Altruism) x 4 items = 4 items
- Total: 100 items
- Recommended for most research purposes

### HEXACO-60 (Short Version)
- 6 domains x 10 items = 60 items (2-3 items per facet)
- No interstitial Altruism scale
- Suitable for time-constrained assessments
- Items selected from the 100-item version to maximize domain-level representation

## Psychometric Properties

### Internal Consistency (Cronbach's Alpha) for HEXACO-60

Data from Ashton & Lee (2009):

| Domain | College Sample | Community Sample |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Honesty-Humility | .77 | .76 |
| Emotionality | .78 | .73 |
| eXtraversion | .80 | .73 |
| Agreeableness | .77 | .75 |
| Conscientiousness | .78 | .76 |
| Openness | .77 | .80 |
| **Range** | **.77-.80** | **.73-.80** |

### Comparison with HEXACO-100

| Domain | HEXACO-100 College | HEXACO-100 Community |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Range | .88-.91 | .87-.90 |

Note: The reduction in reliability from 100-item to 60-item is expected due to shorter scale length. The HEXACO-60 maintains acceptable reliability for domain-level assessment.

### Correlation Between HEXACO-60 and HEXACO-100 Scales

The HEXACO-60 domain scores correlate very highly with the corresponding HEXACO-100 domain scores (r > .90 in most cases), indicating that the shorter version provides a good approximation of the full-length version at the domain level.

### Cross-Cultural Validation

The HEXACO-60 has been validated in multiple languages and cultures:
- German version: Cronbach's alpha .73-.82 (self-report)
- Polish version: Cronbach's alpha .77-.82
- Icelandic version: Confirmed six-factor structure via CFA
- Lithuanian version: Cronbach's alpha .67-.80
- Arabic version: Validated against BFI-2
- Cross-cultural study (18 countries): Confirmed structural stability of the six-factor model (Garcia et al., 2022)

### Test-Retest Reliability (HEXACO-100)

For the full 100-item version (Lee & Ashton, 2018), test-retest reliability over several weeks ranged from .85 to .91 across the six domains.

## Key Differences from the Big Five (FFM)

The HEXACO model differs from the Big Five (FFM) in several important ways:

1. **Additional dimension:** The HEXACO adds Honesty-Humility (H) as a sixth factor, which is not represented in the Big Five.

2. **Emotionality vs. Neuroticism:** HEXACO Emotionality (E) includes sentimentality and dependence content that is not part of Big Five Neuroticism; conversely, Big Five Neuroticism includes anger/irritability content that the HEXACO places in Agreeableness.

3. **Agreeableness rotation:** HEXACO Agreeableness (A) includes patience/anger content (from Big Five Neuroticism) and excludes sentimentality content (moved to Emotionality). This represents a rotation of the Big Five Agreeableness-Neuroticism space.

4. **Openness:** The HEXACO Openness factor is similar to Big Five Openness/Intellect but with a stronger emphasis on unconventionality.
