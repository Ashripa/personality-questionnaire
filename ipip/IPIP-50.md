## Instructions / 指导语

**English:**
Describe yourself as you generally are now, not as you wish to be in the future. Describe yourself as you honestly see yourself, in relation to other people you know of the same sex as you are, and roughly your same age. Indicate for each statement how accurately it describes you, using the following scale:

1 = Very Inaccurate
2 = Moderately Inaccurate
3 = Neither Accurate Nor Inaccurate
4 = Moderately Accurate
5 = Very Accurate

**Chinese / 中文:**
请描述你现在通常的样子，而非你希望将来成为的样子。请诚实地描述你自己，与你所知的同性别、大致同龄的人相比。请用以下量表指出每项陈述对你的描述有多准确：

1 = 非常不准确
2 = 比较不准确
3 = 既不准确也不是不准确
4 = 比较准确
5 = 非常准确

## Scoring Instructions / 计分方法

### Response Scale / 作答量表

| Value | English | Chinese |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Very Inaccurate | 非常不准确 |
| 2 | Moderately Inaccurate | 比较不准确 |
| 3 | Neither Accurate Nor Inaccurate | 既不准确也不是不准确 |
| 4 | Moderately Accurate | 比较准确 |
| 5 | Very Accurate | 非常准确 |

### Reverse Scoring / 反向计分

For items marked with Key = "-" (reverse-scored), recode responses as follows:

| Original | Recoded |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 |

Formula: Recoded = 6 - Original

### Computing Factor Scores (IPIP-50) / 计算因子得分

After reverse-coding the negatively keyed items, sum the 10 items per factor. Score range per factor: 10-50.

| Factor | Positively Keyed (+) | Reverse-Scored (-) |
|--------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Extraversion | 1, 11, 21, 31, 41 | 6, 16, 26, 36, 46 |
| Agreeableness | 7, 17, 27, 37, 42, 47 | 2, 12, 22, 32 |
| Conscientiousness | 3, 13, 23, 33, 43, 48 | 8, 18, 28, 38 |
| Emotional Stability | 9, 19 | 4, 14, 24, 29, 34, 39, 44, 49 |
| Intellect/Openness | 5, 15, 25, 35, 40, 45, 50 | 10, 20, 30 |

### Computing Factor Scores (Mini-IPIP-20) / 计算因子得分

After reverse-coding, sum the 4 items per factor. Score range per factor: 4-20.

| Factor | Items (Admin #) | Positively Keyed | Reverse-Scored |
|--------|-----------------|------------------|----------------|
| Extraversion | 1, 6, 11, 16 | 1, 11 | 6, 16 |
| Agreeableness | 2, 7, 12, 17 | 2, 12 | 7, 17 |
| Conscientiousness | 3, 8, 13, 18 | 3, 13 | 8, 18 |
| Neuroticism | 4, 9, 14, 19 | 4, 14 | 9, 19 |
| Intellect/Openness | 5, 10, 15, 20 | 5 | 10, 15, 20 |

**Note on Factor IV direction:** The IPIP-50 labels Factor IV as "Emotional Stability" (high score = calm, stable). The Mini-IPIP labels it as "Neuroticism" (high score = anxious, moody). When computing Neuroticism, items like "Am relaxed most of the time" and "Seldom feel blue" are reverse-scored. When computing Emotional Stability, items like "Have frequent mood swings" and "Get upset easily" are reverse-scored. The items are identical; only the polarity label changes.

## Psychometric Properties / 心理测量学指标

### IPIP-50 (Goldberg, 1992)

Typical internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) across studies:

| Factor | Alpha |
|--------|-------|
| Extraversion | .87 |
| Agreeableness | .82 |
| Conscientiousness | .79 |
| Emotional Stability | .86 |
| Intellect/Openness | .84 |

### Mini-IPIP-20 (Donnellan et al., 2006)

Typical internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha):

| Factor | Alpha range |
|--------|-------------|
| Extraversion | .77-.82 |
| Agreeableness | .70-.75 |
| Conscientiousness | .69-.75 |
| Neuroticism | .68-.74 |
| Intellect/Openness | .65-.73 |
