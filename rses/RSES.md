# Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale (RSES)

**Author:** Morris Rosenberg

**Citation:** Rosenberg, M. (1965). *Society and the adolescent self-image.* Princeton University Press.

**Purpose:** A 10-item scale measuring global self-esteem. Originally designed for high school students, now widely used across all age groups. One of the most cited self-esteem instruments in the social sciences.

---

## Instructions

Below is a list of statements dealing with your general feelings about yourself. Please indicate how strongly you agree or disagree with each statement.

**Response options:**

| Code | English | Chinese |
|------|---------|---------|
| SA | Strongly Agree | 非常符合 |
| A | Agree | 符合 |
| D | Disagree | 不符合 |
| SD | Strongly Disagree | 很不符合 |

---

## Items / 条目

Items are listed in the original Rosenberg (1965) ordering, which is also the ordering used in the standard Chinese adaptation.

| # | English | Chinese | Direction |
|---|---------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | I feel that I'm a person of worth, at least on an equal plane with others. | 我感到我是一个有价值的人，至少与其他人在同一水平上。 | Positive |
| 2 | I feel that I have a number of good qualities. | 我感到我有许多好的品质。 | Positive |
| 3 | All in all, I am inclined to feel that I am a failure. | 归根结底，我倾向于觉得自己是一个失败者。 | **Negative (R)** |
| 4 | I am able to do things as well as most other people. | 我能像大多数人一样把事情做好。 | Positive |
| 5 | I feel I do not have much to be proud of. | 我感到自己值得自豪的地方不多。 | **Negative (R)** |
| 6 | I take a positive attitude toward myself. | 我对自己持肯定的态度。 | Positive |
| 7 | On the whole, I am satisfied with myself. | 总的来说，我对自己是满意的。 | Positive |
| 8 | I wish I could have more respect for myself. | 我希望能为自己赢得更多尊重。 | See note below |
| 9 | I certainly feel useless at times. | 我确实时常感到自己毫无用处。 | **Negative (R)** |
| 10 | At times I think I am no good at all. | 我时常认为自己一无是处。 | **Negative (R)** |

**(R)** = Reverse-scored item.

### Note on Item 8

Item 8 is traditionally classified as a negatively worded item and reverse-scored in the original English version. However, in many Chinese adaptations (including the widely used version introduced by Wang Xiangdong et al.), item 8 is scored as a **positive** item because the Chinese phrasing ("希望能为自己赢得更多尊重") reads more as a healthy aspiration than a self-esteem deficit. Researchers using the Chinese version should specify which scoring convention they follow and cite accordingly.

## Scoring

### Likert Scoring Method (most common)

This is the simpler and more widely used scoring approach.

**Positive items (1, 2, 4, 6, 7):**

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Strongly Agree | 4 |
| Agree | 3 |
| Disagree | 2 |
| Strongly Disagree | 1 |

**Reverse-scored items (3, 5, 8, 9, 10) -- or (3, 5, 9, 10) if using Chinese convention for item 8:**

| Response | Score |
|----------|-------|
| Strongly Agree | 1 |
| Agree | 2 |
| Disagree | 3 |
| Strongly Disagree | 4 |

**Total score** = sum of all 10 items.

- Range: **10--40** (using 1--4 scoring)
- Higher scores indicate higher self-esteem.

> Some researchers use 0--3 scoring instead of 1--4, yielding a total range of 0--30. The interpretation is the same; only the numerical range shifts.

### Guttman Scoring Method (original)

The original Guttman-scale scoring uses combined ratings (see APA reference document for details). The Likert method above is far more commonly used in contemporary research.

---

## Interpretation Guidelines

Using the **1--4 Likert scoring** (range 10--40):

| Score Range | Interpretation |
|-------------|---------------|
| 30--40 | **High self-esteem** (normal, healthy range) |
| 20--29 | **Moderate self-esteem** (within typical range but may indicate some self-esteem concerns) |
| 10--19 | **Low self-esteem** (suggests clinically meaningful self-esteem difficulties) |

Using the **0--3 Likert scoring** (range 0--30):

| Score Range | Interpretation |
|-------------|---------------|
| 25--30 | High self-esteem |
| 15--24 | Moderate self-esteem |
| 0--14 | Low self-esteem |

> Note: These cutoffs are general guidelines. Scores should be interpreted in context, and no single cutoff definitively separates "normal" from "low" self-esteem. Normative data vary across populations, cultures, and age groups.

---

## Psychometric Properties

- **Internal consistency:** Guttman scale coefficient of reproducibility = .92
- **Test-retest reliability:** r = .85--.88 over 2 weeks
- **Validity:** Demonstrates concurrent, predictive, and construct validity; correlates significantly with other self-esteem measures (e.g., Coopersmith Self-Esteem Inventory) and in the expected direction with measures of depression and anxiety

## APA Note on Item Ordering

The APA's published version of the scale (available at apa.org) uses a different item ordering from the original 1965 publication. In the APA version, the items are reordered as follows (mapping to original numbers): 7, 10, 2, 4, 5, 9, 1, 8, 3, 6. The items themselves are identical; only the presentation order differs. This document uses the original Rosenberg (1965) ordering, consistent with the standard Chinese adaptation.
