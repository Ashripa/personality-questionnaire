# Attributional Style Questionnaire (ASQ)

## APA Citation

Peterson, C., Semmel, A., von Baeyer, C., Abramson, L. Y., Metalsky, G. I., & Seligman, M. E. P. (1982). The Attributional Style Questionnaire. *Cognitive Therapy and Research, 6*(3), 287--300. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01173577

---

## Overview

The Attributional Style Questionnaire (ASQ) is a self-report instrument designed to measure individual differences in **explanatory style** (also called attributional style). It presents 12 hypothetical events and asks respondents to identify the major cause and then rate that cause along three causal dimensions. The ASQ was developed within the framework of the **reformulated learned helplessness model** of depression (Abramson, Seligman, & Teasdale, 1978).

---

## Instructions (English)

> Please try to vividly imagine yourself in the situations that follow. If such a situation happened to you, what would you feel would have caused it? While events may have many causes, we want you to pick only **one -- the major cause** if this event happened to you. Please write this cause in the blank provided after each event. Next, we ask you to answer some questions about the cause, using the scales provided. There are no right or wrong answers. Please circle one number on each scale.

---

## The 12 Hypothetical Events

### Classification

The 12 events are classified along two dimensions:

|  | Interpersonal / Affiliative | Achievement |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Positive** (Good events) | 3 events | 3 events |
| **Negative** (Bad events) | 3 events | 3 events |

### All 12 Events

Based on the standard version (Peterson et al., 1982; Peterson & Villanova, 1988), the 12 events are:

> **Note:** The exact wording and event-valence assignment can vary slightly across published editions and translations. Some versions present events in a different order. The table below follows the most commonly cited version. Researchers should consult the original paper or the Positive Psychology Center (University of Pennsylvania) for the definitive authoritative version.

**Positive (Good) Events:**

| Event # | Domain | Event |
|--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | Achievement | You become very rich. |
| 2 | Achievement | You apply for a position that you want very badly (e.g., important job, graduate school admission, etc.), and you get it. |
| 3 | Interpersonal | You meet a friend who compliments you on your appearance. |
| 4 | Achievement | You get a raise. |
| 5 | Interpersonal | Your spouse (boyfriend/girlfriend) has been treating you more lovingly. |
| 6 | Interpersonal | You do a project that is highly praised. |

**Negative (Bad) Events:**

| Event # | Domain | Event |
|--:|:--|:--|
| 7 | Achievement | You have been looking for a job unsuccessfully for some time. |
| 8 | Achievement | You give an important talk in front of a group, and the audience reacts negatively. |
| 9 | Achievement | You can't get all the work done that others expect of you. |
| 10 | Interpersonal | You go out on a date, and it goes badly. |
| 11 | Interpersonal | A friend comes to you with a problem, and you don't try to help him/her. |
| 12 | Interpersonal | You put on a special dinner for a friend, but it turns out badly. |

## Three Causal Dimension Rating Scales

After writing the one major cause for each event, respondents rate that cause on three 7-point scales:

### 1. Internality (Locus of Causality)

> Is the cause of [event] due to something about you, or something about other people or circumstances?

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Totally due to other people or circumstances | | | | | | Totally due to me |

### 2. Stability

> In the future, when [similar event occurs], will this cause again be present?

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Will never again be present | | | | | | Will always be present |

### 3. Globality

> Is the cause something that just influences [this specific area], or does it also influence other areas of your life?

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Influences just this particular situation | | | | | | Influences all situations in my life |

### Optional: Importance Rating

Some versions include a fourth question about the perceived importance of the event:

> If this situation happened to you, how important would it be to you?

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Not at all important | | | | | | Extremely important |

---

## Reverse-Scoring

There are **no reverse-scored items** per se. However, the scoring uses the natural direction of the scales:

- For **negative events**, higher Internality/Stability/Globality scores indicate a more **pessimistic** (depressogenic) explanatory style.
- For **positive events**, higher Internality/Stability/Globality scores indicate a more **optimistic** explanatory style.

---

## Scoring Method

### Step 1: Compute Dimension Scores for Each Event

Each event yields three scores (Internality, Stability, Globality), each on a 1--7 scale.

### Step 2: Compute Composite Scores

| Composite Score | Formula | Range |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **CoNeg** (Composite Negative) | Mean of all 18 ratings (3 dimensions x 6 negative events) | 1.00 -- 7.00 |
| **CoPos** (Composite Positive) | Mean of all 18 ratings (3 dimensions x 6 positive events) | 1.00 -- 7.00 |
| **CPCN** (Overall Explanatory Style) | CoPos minus CoNeg | -6.00 to +6.00 |

### Step 3: Compute Dimension-Specific Scores (Optional)

| Score | Formula |
|:--|:--|
| **Internal-Negative** | Mean of Internality ratings across 6 negative events |
| **Stable-Negative** | Mean of Stability ratings across 6 negative events |
| **Global-Negative** | Mean of Globality ratings across 6 negative events |
| **Internal-Positive** | Mean of Internality ratings across 6 positive events |
| **Stable-Positive** | Mean of Stability ratings across 6 positive events |
| **Global-Positive** | Mean of Globality ratings across 6 positive events |

### Interpretation

| Score | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| High CoNeg | Pessimistic explanatory style for bad events (internal, stable, global causes for failures) |
| Low CoNeg | Optimistic explanatory style for bad events |
| High CoPos | Optimistic explanatory style for good events (internal, stable, global causes for successes) |
| High CPCN | More optimistic overall explanatory style |
| Low/negative CPCN | More pessimistic overall explanatory style |

---

## Psychometric Properties

| Property | Values |
|:--|:--|
| Internal consistency (alpha) | CoNeg: .72; CoPos: .75; Individual dimensions: .44 -- .69 |
| Test-retest reliability (5 weeks) | CoNeg: .64; CoPos: .70; CPCN: .69 |
| Convergent validity | CoNeg correlates with BDI depression scores (r = .25 -- .35) |
| Predictive validity | Predicts depression onset, academic performance, physical health, job performance |
| Number of validation studies | Over 500 studies and 100 universities |
| Known limitations | Individual dimension subscales have modest reliability due to only 6 items per dimension x valence combination; some factor analyses yield event-specific rather than dimension-specific factors |

## Chinese Version

A Chinese translation of the ASQ has been used in research in mainland China and Taiwan. Below is a commonly referenced translation of the 12 events:

**Positive Events:**

| Event # | Domain | Chinese |
|--:|:--|:--|
| 1 | Achievement | 你变得非常富有。 |
| 2 | Achievement | 你申请了一个你非常想要的职位（如重要工作、研究生入学等），而且你被录取了。 |
| 3 | Interpersonal | 你遇到了一个朋友，他/她赞美了你的外表。 |
| 4 | Achievement | 你得到了加薪。 |
| 5 | Interpersonal | 你的配偶（男/女朋友）一直对你更加温柔体贴。 |
| 6 | Interpersonal | 你做了一个项目，受到了高度赞扬。 |

**Negative Events:**

| Event # | Domain | Chinese |
|--:|:--|:--|
| 7 | Achievement | 你已经找了很长时间的工作，但一直没有成功。 |
| 8 | Achievement | 你在一群人面前做了一次重要的演讲，但听众反应很差。 |
| 9 | Achievement | 你无法完成别人期望你完成的所有工作。 |
| 10 | Interpersonal | 你出去约会了，但约会进行得很糟糕。 |
| 11 | Interpersonal | 一个朋友带着问题来找你，但你没有试着帮助他/她。 |
| 12 | Interpersonal | 你为朋友精心准备了一顿晚餐，但结果很糟糕。 |

**Causal Dimension Scales (Chinese):**

| Dimension | Left Anchor (1) | Right Anchor (7) |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 内在性 (Internality) | 完全由于他人或环境 | 完全由于我自己 |
| 稳定性 (Stability) | 以后不会再出现 | 以后总会出现 |
| 全面性 (Globality) | 仅影响这一特定情境 | 影响我生活的所有方面 |

> **Note:** Chinese versions have been used in studies published in Chinese psychology journals (e.g., *Acta Psychologica Sinica*, *Chinese Journal of Clinical Psychology*). Internal consistency for the Chinese version: alpha = .72 -- .74; 5-week test-retest reliability: .57 -- .69. Researchers should obtain validated versions from published Chinese-language studies or contact the Positive Psychology Center at the University of Pennsylvania.
