## Overview

The Brief Resilience Scale (BRS) is a 6-item self-report measure designed to assess the ability to **bounce back or recover from stress**. Unlike many resilience scales that measure protective factors (e.g., social support, optimism), the BRS directly measures the core construct of resilience -- the capacity to recover from adversity. It contains 3 positively worded items and 3 negatively worded (reverse-scored) items.

## Items

| # | English | Direction | Reverse-scored? |
|:-:|:--------|:---------:|:---------------:|
| 1 | I tend to bounce back quickly after hard times. | Positive | No |
| 2 | I have a hard time making it through stressful events. | Negative | **Yes** |
| 3 | It does not take me long to recover from a stressful event. | Positive | No |
| 4 | It is hard for me to snap back when something bad happens. | Negative | **Yes** |
| 5 | I usually come through difficult times with little trouble. | Positive | No |
| 6 | I tend to take a long time to get over set-backs in my life. | Negative | **Yes** |

## Scoring Method

1. Reverse-score items 2, 4, and 6.
2. Calculate the **mean** of all 6 items (after reverse coding).
3. Score range: **1.00 -- 5.00**.
4. Higher scores indicate greater resilience (better ability to bounce back from stress).

### Interpretation Guidelines (Smith et al., 2008)

| Score Range | Interpretation |
|:------------|:---------------|
| 1.00 -- 2.99 | Low resilience |
| 3.00 -- 4.30 | Normal resilience |
| 4.31 -- 5.00 | High resilience |

Average score in the original development studies: **M = 3.70**.

## Psychometric Properties

### Original Study (Smith et al., 2008)

| Property | Value |
|:---------|:------|
| Internal consistency | alpha = .80--.91 (across 4 samples) |
| Test-retest reliability (1 month) | r = .69 |
| Test-retest reliability (3 months) | r = .62 |
| Factor structure | Unidimensional (single factor) |

- Convergent validity: Positively correlated with optimism, purpose in life, social support, and active coping.
- Discriminant validity: Negatively correlated with pessimism, alexithymia, negative affect, and behavioral disengagement.
- Predictive validity: Predicted anxiety, depression, negative affect, and physical symptoms after controlling for other resilience resources.

### Chinese Version (Lai & Yue, 2014)

| Property | Hong Kong Sample | Mainland (Nanjing) Sample |
|:---------|:----------------|:-------------------------|
| Sample size | n = 547 | n = 268 |
| Cronbach's alpha | .76 | .72 |
| Eigenvalue (factor 1) | 2.80 (46.73% variance) | 2.53 (42.18% variance) |
| Factor structure | Single factor | Single factor |

### Additional Chinese Validation (Ye et al., 2020)

| Property | Value |
|:---------|:------|
| Sample | Older adults with chronic illness |
| Cronbach's alpha | .78 |
