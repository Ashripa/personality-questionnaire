# Buss-Perry Aggression Questionnaire (AQ)

## Citation (APA 7th Edition)

Buss, A. H., & Perry, M. (1992). The Aggression Questionnaire. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, *63*(3), 452-459. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.63.3.452

---

## Overview

The Aggression Questionnaire (AQ) is a 29-item self-report instrument designed to measure aggression in adults (18+). It assesses four dimensions of aggression: Physical Aggression, Verbal Aggression, Anger, and Hostility. The AQ was developed as a revision and improvement of the earlier Buss-Durkee Hostility Inventory (BDHI; Buss & Durkee, 1957).

---

## Instructions to Participants

Using the 5-point scale shown below, indicate how uncharacteristic or characteristic each of the following statements is in describing you. Place your rating in the box to the right of the statement.

| Rating | Label |
|--------|-------|
| 1 | Extremely uncharacteristic of me |
| 2 | Somewhat uncharacteristic of me |
| 3 | Neither uncharacteristic nor characteristic of me |
| 4 | Somewhat characteristic of me |
| 5 | Extremely characteristic of me |

### Subscale Legend

- **[PA]** = Physical Aggression
- **[VA]** = Verbal Aggression
- **[A]** = Anger
- **[H]** = Hostility
- **(R)** = Reverse-scored item

## Scoring Key

### Subscale Item Composition

| Subscale | Items | Number of Items | Score Range |
|----------|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Physical Aggression (PA) | 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, **24(R)**, 27, 29 | 9 | 9 - 45 |
| Verbal Aggression (VA) | 2, 6, 10, 14, 18 | 5 | 5 - 25 |
| Anger (A) | 3, 7, 11, **15(R)**, 19, 22, 25 | 7 | 7 - 35 |
| Hostility (H) | 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, 26, 28 | 8 | 8 - 40 |
| **Total Aggression** | **All 29 items** | **29** | **29 - 145** |

### Reverse Scoring

Two items are worded in the direction opposite to aggression and must be reverse-scored before computing subscale and total scores:

- **Item 15**: "I am an even-tempered person." (Anger subscale)
- **Item 24**: "I can think of no good reason for ever hitting a person." (Physical Aggression subscale)

Reverse scoring conversion:

| Original Response | Reversed Score |
|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | 5 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 |

### Scoring Procedure

1. **Reverse-score** items 15 and 24 using the conversion table above.
2. **Calculate subscale scores** by summing the ratings for items belonging to each subscale.
3. **Calculate the Total Aggression score** by summing all four subscale scores (or equivalently, summing all 29 items after reverse scoring).
4. Higher scores indicate higher levels of aggression.

### Note on Item Order

Buss and Perry (1992) recommend that investigators "scramble the items so that items from any one factor do not pile up." The order presented above interleaves items from different subscales as in the original published appendix.

## Chinese Version / 中文版

### Version A: AQ-CV (Li Xianyun et al., 2011) -- 30-item adaptation

**Citation:**
李献云, 费立鹏, 张亚利, 牛雅娟, 童永胜, 杨少杰. (2011). Buss和Perry攻击问卷中文版的修订和信效度. *中国神经精神疾病杂志*, *37*(10), 607-613.

This Chinese adaptation (AQ-CV) expanded the original 29-item scale to **30 items** and added a fifth subscale: **self-directed aggression** (指向自我的攻击性). It was validated with 602 rural residents, 546 urban residents, and 628 university students aged 18+. Internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) ranged from 0.60-0.89; test-retest reliability (ICC) from 0.57-0.81.

**Five subscales:** 身体攻击性 (Physical Aggression), 言语攻击性 (Verbal Aggression), 愤怒 (Anger), 敌意 (Hostility), 指向自我的攻击性 (Self-directed Aggression)

**Response options (5-point):**

| 分值 | 选项 |
|------|------|
| 1 | 不符合 |
| 2 | 较少符合 |
| 3 | 一半符合 |
| 4 | 基本符合 |
| 5 | 完全符合 |

**Complete AQ-CV 30 Items:**

1. 某些情况下，我会控制不住而打人。
2. 我不同意朋友意见时，就当面反对。
3. 我的脾气一点就着，但一会儿就好。
4. 我的嫉妒心较强。
5. 很烦躁时我会想到伤害自己。*
6. 如果有人故意找我麻烦，严重时我会揍他。
7. 我喜欢否定他人的意见。
8. 事情不顺利时我的烦躁会表现出来。
9. 我觉得我遇到不公平的事较多。
10. 很生气时我会因不小心而受伤。*
11. 如果有人打我，我会还击。
12. 当人们干扰我时，我会毫不客气地指责他们。
13. 我生气时感觉象个火药库，随时会爆炸。
14. 陌生人对我过于友好时，我怀疑对方另有目的。
15. 特别激动时我会忽视自身安全。*
16. 我比别人打架稍多一些。
17. 当人们与我意见不同时，我会忍不住与其争论。
18. 我难以控制自己的脾气。
19. 我对某些事情感到耿耿于怀。
20. 很自责时我会惩罚自己。*
21. 必要时我用武力维护自己的权利。
22. 我容易与人发生争吵。
23. 看到不顺眼的事，我容易发火。
24. 我认为有"朋友"说我的坏话。
25. 情绪不好时我会做出诸如大量吸烟、喝酒或不注意饮食等危害自身健康的行为。*
26. 如果周围人为难我到一定程度，我和他们会动手打架。
27. 我会无缘无故发脾气。
28. 当别人对我特别好时，我觉得他们有所企图。
29. 我很生气时，会当他人面摔东西。
30. 我怀疑有人在背后嘲笑我。

> \* Items 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 are the newly added self-directed aggression items (指向自我的攻击性), not present in the original 29-item English version.

### Version B: Other Chinese Adaptations

- **Hong Kong Chinese version**: Maxwell, J. P. (2007). Development and preliminary validation of a Chinese version of the Buss-Perry Aggression Questionnaire in a population of Hong Kong Chinese. *Journal of Personality Assessment*, *88*(3), 284-294.

- **Chinese college student version (CC-BPAQ)**: A 22-item, 4-subscale version (hostility, physical aggression, impulsivity, anger) validated with 1,722 college students from 9 universities. Cronbach's alpha = 0.89; test-retest reliability = 0.91. Published in *Chinese Mental Health Journal* (中国心理卫生杂志), 2013.

- **Chinese adolescent version**: Liu Junsheng (刘俊升) revised a 20-item youth version in 2009 with 4 dimensions: physical aggression, hostility, anger, and substitute aggression.

## Psychometric Properties (Original English Version)

From Buss & Perry (1992):

| Subscale | Cronbach's Alpha | Test-Retest Reliability |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Physical Aggression | .85 | .80 |
| Verbal Aggression | .72 | .76 |
| Anger | .83 | .72 |
| Hostility | .77 | .72 |

Test-retest interval: 9 weeks. Sample: 1,253 university students (612 males, 641 females).
