# PsyCap / PCQ — Psychological Capital Questionnaire

## APA Citation

Luthans, F., Youssef, C. M., & Avolio, B. J. (2007). *Psychological capital: Developing the human competitive edge*. Oxford, UK: Oxford University Press.

Luthans, F., Avolio, B. J., Avey, J. B., & Norman, S. M. (2007). Positive psychological capital: Measurement and relationship with performance and satisfaction. *Personnel Psychology, 60*(3), 541–572.

---

## Overview

The Psychological Capital Questionnaire (PCQ) measures **Psychological Capital (PsyCap)**, a higher-order positive psychological resource comprising four dimensions known as the **HERO model**: Hope, (Self-)Efficacy, Resilience, and Optimism. PsyCap is defined as "an individual's positive psychological state of development."

Two validated versions exist:

| Version | Items | Items per subscale | Administration time |
|---------|:-----:|:------------------:|:-------------------:|
| PCQ-24  | 24    | 6                  | 10–15 min           |
| PCQ-12  | 12    | 3                  | 5–10 min            |

---

## Scale Structure (HERO Model)

### PCQ-24: Four Subscales (6 items each)

| Subscale | Items | Theoretical Origin | Core Construct |
|----------|:-----:|---------------------|---------------|
| **Self-Efficacy** (items 1–6) | 6 | Parker (1998) Role Breadth Self-Efficacy Scale | Confidence in taking on and putting in effort for challenging tasks |
| **Hope** (items 7–12) | 6 | Snyder et al. (1996) State Hope Scale | Willpower (agency) and pathways to achieve goals |
| **Resilience** (items 13–18) | 6 | Wagnild & Young (1993) Resilience Scale | Capacity to bounce back from adversity |
| **Optimism** (items 19–24) | 6 | Scheier & Carver (1985) Life Orientation Test | Positive attribution about succeeding now and in the future |

### PCQ-12: Short Form

- 3 items per subscale (12 items total), selected from the PCQ-24
- Reverse-scored items removed to improve reliability of Resilience and Optimism subscales
- Suitable for repeated-measures and diary study designs

### Scoring

- **Subscale scores:** Mean of items within each subscale
- **Total PsyCap score:** Mean (or sum) of all 24 (or 12) items; treated as a **higher-order construct** in most research
- Higher scores = greater psychological capital

---

## Response Format

6-point Likert scale:
1 = *Strongly disagree*
2 = *Disagree*
3 = *Somewhat disagree*
4 = *Somewhat agree*
5 = *Agree*
6 = *Strongly agree*

**Note:** Some items are **reverse-scored** (particularly in the Resilience and Optimism subscales of the PCQ-24). The PCQ-12 drops these items.

---

## Administration

- **Mode:** Paper-and-pencil or online survey
- **Population:** Working adults; also adapted for students, nurses, teachers
- **Context:** Primarily organizational behavior / positive organizational psychology research
- **Can be adapted:** Stem "At my workplace..." can be modified to "At my school..." for student samples

---

## Psychometric Properties

### PCQ-24
- **Internal consistency:** Cronbach's alpha = .88–.89 (total); subscale alphas typically .72–.85
- **Factor structure:** Four-factor model (Hope, Efficacy, Resilience, Optimism) and higher-order single-factor model both supported via CFA
- **Test-retest reliability:** Moderate stability expected given PsyCap is conceptualized as a **state-like** construct (malleable, not fixed trait)
- **Convergent validity:** Correlates with job satisfaction, organizational commitment, work engagement, and job performance (meta-analytic r = .26 with performance)
- **Discriminant validity:** Distinct from Big Five personality traits and core self-evaluations

### PCQ-12
- **Internal consistency:** Alpha = .80–.85 (total score)
- **Improved subscale reliability:** Dropping reverse-scored items solved weak alpha for Resilience and Optimism
- **Known issue:** Bifactor model suggests a dominant general PsyCap factor with relatively weak specific subscale factors

## Sample Items (from published validation articles)

1. "I feel confident analyzing a long-term problem to find a solution." *(Self-Efficacy)*
2. "I can think of many ways to reach my current work goals." *(Hope)*
3. "I usually manage difficulties one way or another at work." *(Resilience)*

*(These items appear in multiple published PCQ validation studies and the Wikipedia entry for the PCQ.)*

## Chinese Version Status

- The **Chinese version of the PCQ-24** has been **widely used** in mainland China, particularly among enterprise employees, teachers, and nurses.
- **Reliability:** Cronbach's alpha over .80 reported in multiple Chinese samples (Qingshan et al., 2014; various nursing studies).
- **Factor structure:** Four-factor HERO model generally replicated in Chinese samples, though bifactor models also fit well.
- A 2025 study (Springer) specifically examined the dimensionality and psychometric properties of the **Chinese PCQ-12 (CPCQ-12)**, comparing single-factor, four-factor, higher-order, and bifactor CFA models.
- **Cultural adaptation note:** Some researchers have developed indigenous Chinese PsyCap scales that add culturally relevant items (e.g., guanxi efficacy, harmony orientation) beyond the standard HERO dimensions.
