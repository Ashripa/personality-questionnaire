# Defense Style Questionnaire-40 (DSQ-40)

## Citation

Andrews, G., Singh, M., & Bond, M. (1993). The Defense Style Questionnaire. *Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease, 181*(4), 246-256. https://doi.org/10.1097/00005053-199304000-00006

## Overview

The DSQ-40 is a 40-item self-report questionnaire measuring conscious derivatives of 20 unconscious defense mechanisms, grouped into three hierarchical defense styles: **Mature**, **Neurotic**, and **Immature**. It was derived from the earlier 88-item DSQ (Bond, 1983) and 72-item DSQ (Andrews et al., 1989), retaining 34 original items and adding 3 new items (for Rationalization and Autistic Fantasy). Each defense mechanism is assessed by 2 items.

## Instructions

> This questionnaire consists of a number of statements about personal attitudes. There are no right or wrong answers. Using the 9-point scale shown below, please indicate how much you agree or disagree with each statement. For example, a score of 5 would indicate that you neither agree nor disagree with the statement, a score of 3 that you moderately disagree, a score of 9 that you strongly agree.

|---------------|---------------------|-------|-------------|
| **Mature** | 1-4 (Sublimation, Humor, Anticipation, Suppression) | 2, 3, 5, 25, 26, 30, 35, 38 (8 items) | Mean of the 4 mechanism scores |

### Reverse Scoring

There are **no reverse-scored items** in the DSQ-40. All items are scored in the keyed direction (1-9).

### Criterion Validity

Andrews et al. (1993) demonstrated:
- Clinical groups (anxiety disorder, abusive parents) scored significantly higher on Immature defenses and lower on Mature defenses compared to community controls.
- Mature defense style negatively correlated with depression (BDI) and anxiety (TAS) symptoms.
- Immature defense style positively correlated with depression and anxiety symptoms.
- Neurotic defense style positively correlated with anxiety symptoms.
