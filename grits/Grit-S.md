# Grit Scales: Grit-S (Short) & Grit-O (Original)

## 1. Short Grit Scale (Grit-S)

### Citation

Duckworth, A. L., & Quinn, P. D. (2009). Development and validation of the Short Grit Scale (Grit-S). *Journal of Personality Assessment*, *91*(2), 166-174. https://doi.org/10.1080/00223890802634290

### Overview

- 8 items, 2 subscales (4 items each)
- 5-point Likert scale
- Derived from the 12-item Grit-O by removing the 2 weakest items per subscale
- Correlation with Grit-O: r = .96

### Instructions

> Directions: Please respond to the following 8 items. Be honest -- there are no right or wrong answers!

### Response Options

| Score | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 5 | Very much like me |
| 4 | Mostly like me |
| 3 | Somewhat like me |
| 2 | Not much like me |
| 1 | Not like me at all |

### Items

| # | Item | Subscale | Scoring |
|---|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | New ideas and projects sometimes distract me from previous ones. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 2 | Setbacks don't discourage me. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 3 | I have been obsessed with a certain idea or project for a short time but later lost interest. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 4 | I am a hard worker. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 5 | I often set a goal but later choose to pursue a different one. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 6 | I have difficulty maintaining my focus on projects that take more than a few months to complete. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 7 | I finish whatever I begin. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 8 | I am diligent. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |

### Scoring Method

1. **Reverse-score** items 1, 3, 5, 6 (CI items): 5->1, 4->2, 3->3, 2->4, 1->5
2. **Consistency of Interest (CI)** = Mean of (reversed) items 1, 3, 5, 6
3. **Perseverance of Effort (PE)** = Mean of items 2, 4, 7, 8
4. **Total Grit** = Mean of all 8 items (after reverse-scoring)
5. Score range: 1 (not at all gritty) to 5 (extremely gritty)

### Psychometric Properties

| Property | Value | Source |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Internal consistency (alpha) | .73 - .83 | Duckworth & Quinn (2009), multiple samples |
| Meta-analytic alpha | .79 | Crede et al., 46 samples |
| PE subscale alpha | .60 - .78 (meta M = .71) | Multiple studies |
| CI subscale alpha | .73 - .79 (meta M = .74) | Multiple studies |
| 1-year test-retest | r = .68 | Adolescent sample |
| Factor structure | Two first-order factors loading on a second-order grit factor | Duckworth & Quinn (2009) |

### Normative Data (Approximate)

- Overall Grit: M ~ 3.4
- Consistency of Interest: M ~ 2.9
- Perseverance of Effort: M ~ 3.7

## 2. Original Grit Scale (Grit-O)

### Citation

Duckworth, A. L., Peterson, C., Matthews, M. D., & Kelly, D. R. (2007). Grit: Perseverance and passion for long-term goals. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, *92*(6), 1087-1101. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.92.6.1087

### Overview

- 12 items, 2 subscales (6 items each)
- 5-point Likert scale
- 6 Consistency of Interest items (all reverse-scored)
- 6 Perseverance of Effort items (all forward-scored)

### Instructions

> Directions: Please respond to the following 12 items. Be honest -- there are no right or wrong answers!

### Response Options

Same as Grit-S (1 = Not like me at all; 5 = Very much like me)

### Items

| # | Item | Subscale | Scoring |
|---|------|----------|---------|
| 1 | I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 2 | New ideas and projects sometimes distract me from previous ones. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 3 | My interests change from year to year. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 4 | Setbacks don't discourage me. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 5 | I have been obsessed with a certain idea or project for a short time but later lost interest. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 6 | I am a hard worker. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 7 | I often set a goal but later choose to pursue a different one. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 8 | I have difficulty maintaining my focus on projects that take more than a few months to complete. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 9 | I finish whatever I begin. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 10 | I have achieved a goal that took years of work. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |
| 11 | I become interested in new pursuits every few months. | Consistency of Interest (CI) | **Reverse** |
| 12 | I am diligent. | Perseverance of Effort (PE) | Forward |

### Scoring Method

1. **Reverse-score** all CI items (2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 11): 5->1, 4->2, 3->3, 2->4, 1->5
2. **Consistency of Interest (CI)** = Mean of (reversed) items 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 11
3. **Perseverance of Effort (PE)** = Mean of items 1, 4, 6, 9, 10, 12
4. **Total Grit** = Mean of all 12 items (after reverse-scoring)
5. Score range: 1 (not at all gritty) to 5 (extremely gritty)

### Psychometric Properties

| Property | Value | Source |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Internal consistency (alpha) - full scale | .85 | Duckworth et al. (2007) |
| PE subscale alpha | .78 | Duckworth et al. (2007) |
| CI subscale alpha | .84 | Duckworth et al. (2007) |
| CFA: CFI (bifactor model) | .96 | Frontiers validation study |
| CFA: TLI (bifactor model) | .94 | Frontiers validation study |
| CFA: RMSEA (bifactor model) | .05 [.033, .070] | Frontiers validation study |
| CFA: SRMR (bifactor model) | .05 | Frontiers validation study |

### Items Removed to Create Grit-S

The following 4 items (2 per subscale) were dropped from Grit-O to create Grit-S:

- **CI items removed**: "My interests change from year to year" (#3) and "I become interested in new pursuits every few months" (#11)
- **PE items removed**: "I have overcome setbacks to conquer an important challenge" (#1) and "I have achieved a goal that took years of work" (#10)

## 5. Key Construct Information

**Grit** is defined as perseverance and passion for long-term goals. It entails working strenuously toward challenges, maintaining effort and interest over years despite failure, adversity, and plateaus in progress. The gritty individual approaches achievement as a marathon; their advantage is stamina. (Duckworth et al., 2007)

**Two Dimensions**:
- **Consistency of Interest (CI)**: The tendency to maintain focus on the same goals and interests over time, rather than frequently changing direction.
- **Perseverance of Effort (PE)**: The tendency to work hard and persist through setbacks, maintaining effort despite difficulties.

**Key Validity Findings**:
- Grit predicts educational attainment, GPA, and retention in challenging programs beyond what IQ alone explains.
- Grit shows positive correlation with conscientiousness (Big Five) but captures a unique aspect of long-term focused tenacity.
- Grit increases with age, suggesting it may develop over the lifespan.
