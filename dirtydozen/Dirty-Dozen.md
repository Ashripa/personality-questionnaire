# Dirty Dozen (DD) -- Dark Triad Dirty Dozen

## APA Citation

Jonason, P. K., & Webster, G. D. (2010). The Dirty Dozen: A concise measure of the Dark Triad. *Psychological Assessment, 22*(2), 420--432. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0019265

**Chinese version:**
Geng, Y. G., Sun, Q. B., Huang, J. Y., Zhu, Y. Z., & Han, X. H. (2015). Dirty Dozen and Short Dark Triad: A Chinese validation of two brief measures of the Dark Triad. *Chinese Journal of Clinical Psychology, 23*(2), 246--250. (耿耀国, 孙群博, 黄婧宜, 朱远征, & 韩晓红)

## Overview

The Dirty Dozen (DD) is a 12-item self-report measure designed to provide a concise assessment of the Dark Triad of personality: **Machiavellianism**, **Psychopathy**, and **Narcissism**. Each construct is measured by 4 items. It was developed as an efficient alternative to the traditional 91-item battery (NPI-40 + MACH-IV + SRP-III).

---

## Instructions

> Please indicate how much you agree with each of the following statements using the scale below.

---

## Response Scale

| Value | Label |
|:-----:|:------|
| 1 | Disagree strongly |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Disagree slightly |
| 4 | Neither agree nor disagree |
| 5 | Agree slightly |
| 6 | Agree |
| 7 | Agree moderately |
| 8 | Agree strongly |
| 9 | Agree very strongly |

> **Note:** The original Jonason & Webster (2010) used a **9-point** scale (1 = disagree strongly to 9 = agree strongly). Some subsequent studies have adapted the scale to 5-point or 7-point formats. The 9-point version is the canonical original.

## Items

### Machiavellianism (Items 1--4)

| # | English | Chinese (中文版) |
|:-:|:--------|:----------------|
| 1 | I tend to manipulate others to get my way. | 我倾向于操纵别人以达到自己的目的。 |
| 2 | I have used deceit or lied to get my way. | 我习惯于欺骗别人以达到自己的目的。 |
| 3 | I have used flattery to get my way. | 我习惯于奉承别人以达到自己的目的。 |
| 4 | I tend to exploit others towards my own end. | 我倾向于利用别人以达到自己的目的。 |

### Psychopathy (Items 5--8)

| # | English | Chinese (中文版) |
|:-:|:--------|:----------------|
| 5 | I tend to lack remorse. | 我缺乏悔恨之心。 |
| 6 | I tend to be unconcerned with the morality of my actions. | 我不太关心自己的行为是否符合道德规范。 |
| 7 | I tend to be callous or insensitive. | 我冷酷、麻木。 |
| 8 | I tend to be cynical. | 我愤世嫉俗。 |

### Narcissism (Items 9--12)

| # | English | Chinese (中文版) |
|:-:|:--------|:----------------|
| 9 | I tend to want others to admire me. | 我希望别人赞美我。 |
| 10 | I tend to want others to pay attention to me. | 我希望别人关注我。 |

---

## Reverse Scoring

**No items are reverse-scored.** All 12 items are positively keyed (higher ratings = higher trait levels).

---

## Scoring Method

1. **Subscale scores:** Calculate the mean (or sum) of the 4 items within each subscale.
   - Machiavellianism = Mean of items 1--4
   - Psychopathy = Mean of items 5--8
   - Narcissism = Mean of items 9--12
2. **Total Dark Triad score:** Calculate the mean (or sum) of all 12 items.
3. Score range per subscale: 4--36 (sum) or 1--9 (mean).
4. Score range total: 12--108 (sum) or 1--9 (mean).
5. Higher scores indicate stronger endorsement of the corresponding dark personality trait.

## Psychometric Properties

### Original Study (Jonason & Webster, 2010)

| Property | Value |
|:---------|:------|
| Internal consistency (total) | alpha = .86 |
| Test-retest reliability (total, 3-week) | r = .89 |
| Machiavellianism alpha | .77--.80 |
| Psychopathy alpha | .63--.77 |
| Narcissism alpha | .72--.78 |

- Confirmatory factor analysis supported a three-factor model.
- Convergent validity: DD subscales correlated significantly with their full-length counterparts (NPI-40, MACH-IV, SRP-III).

### Chinese Version (Geng et al., 2015)

| Property | Value |
|:---------|:------|
| Internal consistency (subscale range) | alpha = .757--.902 |
| Test-retest (6-week, subscale range) | r = .725--.879 |
| CFA fit indices | chi-sq/df = 3.086, RMSEA = .077, NFI = .930, CFI = .951, GFI = .928 |

### Known Limitations

- The narcissism subscale has been noted to have relatively low discriminating power compared to the NPI-40 (Webster & Jonason, 2013).
- Some studies have identified the "exploitation" content as a cross-loading item across Machiavellianism and Psychopathy (Maples et al., 2014).
