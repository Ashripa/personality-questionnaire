# Riso-Hudson Enneagram Type Indicator (RHETI)

**Priority:** P5
**Category:** Typological personality assessment
**Status:** Commercial instrument

---

## Overview

The Riso-Hudson Enneagram Type Indicator (RHETI) is a commercially published forced-choice personality instrument designed to identify an individual's primary Enneagram type among nine personality types. It is the most widely used scientifically validated Enneagram assessment.

## Key Citation

- Riso, D. R., & Hudson, R. (1999). *The Wisdom of the Enneagram: The Complete Guide to Psychological and Spiritual Growth for the Nine Personality Types*. Bantam Books.
- Newgent, R. A., Parr, P. E., Newman, I., & Wiggins, K. K. (2004). The Riso-Hudson Enneagram Type Indicator: Estimates of reliability and validity. *Measurement and Evaluation in Counseling and Development, 36*(4), 226-237.

## Structure

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Items | 144 paired statements (forced-choice) |
| Scales | 9 Enneagram types |
| Format | Forced-choice between two statements per item |
| Administration time | ~40 minutes |
| Age range | Adults |
| Version | RHETI v2.5 (current) |

## The Nine Types

1. **Type 1 - The Reformer:** Principled, purposeful, self-controlled, perfectionistic
2. **Type 2 - The Helper:** Generous, demonstrative, people-pleasing, possessive
3. **Type 3 - The Achiever:** Adaptable, excelling, driven, image-conscious
4. **Type 4 - The Individualist:** Expressive, dramatic, self-absorbed, temperamental
5. **Type 5 - The Investigator:** Perceptive, innovative, secretive, isolated
6. **Type 6 - The Loyalist:** Engaging, responsible, anxious, suspicious
7. **Type 7 - The Enthusiast:** Spontaneous, versatile, acquisitive, scattered
8. **Type 8 - The Challenger:** Self-confident, decisive, willful, confrontational
9. **Type 9 - The Peacemaker:** Receptive, reassuring, complacent, resigned

## Response Format

- Ipsative (forced-choice): respondents choose which of two statements best describes them
- Produces a ranked profile across all nine types rather than absolute scores
- Primary type is identified by the highest score; wing types are adjacent

## Scoring

- Each type receives a score from 0 to 28
- Scores are relative (ipsative), not absolute
- Full profile shows distribution across all nine types
- Identifies primary type, wing, and patterns of type integration/disintegration

## Psychometric Properties

### Reliability
- Internal consistency: Cronbach's alpha ranges from .56 to .82 across different types
- Overall accuracy estimated at ~72%
- Test-retest reliability demonstrated across multiple studies

### Validity
- Three independently conducted validation studies (Newgent, 2001; Giordano, 2008, 2009)
- Convergent validity with NEO PI-R and MBTI reported
- Construct validity supported through factor analysis

### Limitations
- Ipsative scoring limits statistical analyses (cannot use standard parametric tests)
- Some types show lower reliability than others
- Forced-choice format may frustrate respondents who identify with both options
- Limited peer-reviewed validation compared to Big Five instruments
- Enneagram theory itself has contested scientific standing

## Theoretical Background

The Enneagram is a personality typology system with disputed origins, drawing on elements from various spiritual and philosophical traditions. Riso and Hudson systematized it into a modern psychological framework with defined levels of development (healthy, average, unhealthy) for each type, wing theory, and integration/disintegration patterns.

## Access

- **Commercial:** Available through The Enneagram Institute (enneagraminstitute.com)
- **Cost:** Paid assessment (~$12 USD per administration as of 2024)
- **Not open-access:** Items and scoring are proprietary
- **Training:** No formal certification required to administer, but interpretation training is recommended

## Notes

- The RHETI is the most scientifically studied Enneagram instrument but still falls short of the psychometric standards typical of Big Five measures
- Widely used in coaching, organizational development, and spiritual growth contexts
- The ipsative scoring format is both a strength (captures relative preferences) and a limitation (restricts statistical analysis)
