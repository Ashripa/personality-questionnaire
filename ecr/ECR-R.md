# Experiences in Close Relationships-Revised (ECR-R) Questionnaire

## Citation

Fraley, R. C., Waller, N. G., & Brennan, K. A. (2000). An item response theory analysis of self-report measures of adult attachment. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 78*(2), 350-365.

The ECR-R is a revision of Brennan, Clark, and Shaver's (1998) Experiences in Close Relationships (ECR) questionnaire, using Item Response Theory (IRT) to select items with improved measurement precision across the full range of attachment insecurity.

## Scale Overview

- **Items:** 36
- **Dimensions:** 2
  - Attachment Anxiety (Items 1-18): fear of rejection, worry about partner availability and responsiveness
  - Attachment Avoidance (Items 19-36): discomfort with closeness, reluctance to depend on others
- **Response format:** 7-point Likert scale
- **Administration:** Self-report; approximately 10 minutes
- **Population:** Adults (18+) in or reflecting on romantic relationships
- **Availability:** Free for research use (R. Chris Fraley's website)

---

## Instructions

The following statements concern how you feel in emotionally intimate relationships. We are interested in how you generally experience relationships, not just in what is happening in a current relationship. Respond to each statement by indicating how much you agree or disagree with it. Write the number in the space provided, using the following rating scale:

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strongly Disagree | | | Neutral / Mixed | | | Strongly Agree |

## Scoring Instructions

### Step 1: Reverse-score designated items

For reverse-scored items (marked "R" above), recode responses:
1 -> 7, 2 -> 6, 3 -> 5, 4 -> 4, 5 -> 3, 6 -> 2, 7 -> 1

**Anxiety reverse-scored items:** 9, 11
**Avoidance reverse-scored items:** 20, 22, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36

### Step 2: Compute subscale scores

- **Attachment Anxiety score** = Mean of Items 1-18 (after reverse-scoring items 9 and 11)
- **Attachment Avoidance score** = Mean of Items 19-36 (after reverse-scoring items 20, 22, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 33, 34, 35, 36)

### Score range

Each subscale score ranges from 1.00 to 7.00. Higher scores indicate greater attachment anxiety or avoidance, respectively.

### Interpretation

The two dimensions can be combined to approximate four attachment prototypes:

| | Low Avoidance | High Avoidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Low Anxiety** | Secure | Dismissing-Avoidant |
| **High Anxiety** | Preoccupied | Fearful-Avoidant |

A midpoint of 4.0 or median split is commonly used to categorize, though the dimensional approach (retaining continuous scores) is preferred.

---

## Psychometric Properties

- Internal consistency: Cronbach's alpha typically > .90 for both subscales
- Test-retest reliability (3-6 weeks): approximately 85-86% shared variance
- The ECR-R improves on the original ECR by providing more uniform measurement precision across the full range of attachment insecurity (via IRT-based item selection)

## Chinese Version (ECR Chinese Adaptation)

### Important Note on ECR vs. ECR-R in Chinese

The most widely cited Chinese adaptation is Li Tonggui and Kato Kazuo's (2006) translation of the original ECR (Brennan, Clark & Shaver, 1998), not the ECR-R (Fraley et al., 2000). The two instruments differ:

- **ECR (Brennan et al., 1998):** Odd-numbered items = Avoidance, Even-numbered items = Anxiety
- **ECR-R (Fraley et al., 2000):** Items 1-18 = Anxiety, Items 19-36 = Avoidance

A formally published and validated Chinese ECR-R (Fraley et al., 2000) with separate psychometric data is not widely available in the literature. Researchers using the ECR-R in Chinese-language studies have typically translated the items independently.

### Chinese ECR Citation

Li, T. G. (Li Tonggui) & Kato, K. (2006). Measuring adult attachment: Chinese adaptation of the ECR scale [Chengren yilian de celiang: Qinmi guanxi jingli liangbiao (ECR) Zhongwen ban]. *Acta Psychologica Sinica, 38*(3), 399-406.

### Chinese ECR Psychometric Properties

- Sample: 371 Chinese university students
- Internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha): Anxiety = 0.77, Avoidance = 0.82 (later studies report 0.88-0.91)
- Good construct validity and criterion validity confirmed
