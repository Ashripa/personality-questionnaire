# CD-RISC - Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale

## Overview

The Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC) is a self-report measure of resilience developed by Kathryn M. Connor and Jonathan R.T. Davidson (2003). The original 25-item version was later refined by Campbell-Sills and Stein (2007) into a unidimensional 10-item version (CD-RISC-10).

---

## Citation

- **CD-RISC-25 (original):** Connor, K. M., & Davidson, J. R. T. (2003). Development of a new resilience scale: The Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC). *Depression and Anxiety, 18*(2), 76-82. https://doi.org/10.1002/da.10113
- **CD-RISC-10:** Campbell-Sills, L., & Stein, M. B. (2007). Psychometric analysis and refinement of the Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC): Validation of a 10-item measure of resilience. *Journal of Traumatic Stress, 20*(6), 1019-1028. https://doi.org/10.1002/jts.20271
- **Chinese version (CD-RISC-10):** Yu, X., & Zhang, J. (2007). Factor analysis and psychometric evaluation of the Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC) with Chinese people. *Social Behavior and Personality, 35*(1), 19-30.

## Part I: CD-RISC-25 (Original 25-Item Version)

### Structure

The original CD-RISC-25 comprises 25 items rated on a 5-point Likert scale (0-4). The total score ranges from 0 to 100, with higher scores reflecting greater resilience.

### Five-Factor Structure (Connor & Davidson, 2003)

| Factor | Description | No. of Items |
|--------|-------------|:------------:|
| Factor 1 | Personal competence, high standards, and tenacity | 8 |
| Factor 2 | Trust in one's instincts, tolerance of negative affect, and strengthening effects of stress | 7 |
| Factor 3 | Positive acceptance of change and secure relationships | 5 |
| Factor 4 | Control | 3 |
| Factor 5 | Spiritual influences | 2 |

**Note:** Subsequent studies found the five-factor structure to be unstable across independent samples, which motivated the development of the unidimensional CD-RISC-10.

### Response Scale

| Score | Label |
|:-----:|-------|
| 0 | Not true at all |
| 1 | Rarely true |
| 2 | Sometimes true |
| 3 | Often true |
| 4 | True nearly all the time |

### Copyright

The CD-RISC-25 is a copyrighted instrument. Permission for use must be obtained from cd-risc.com. A license fee may apply depending on the context of use (academic, clinical, commercial).

## Part II: CD-RISC-10 (10-Item Version)

### Instructions

Please indicate how much you agree with the following statements as they apply to you **over the last month**. If a particular situation has not occurred recently, answer according to how you think you would have felt. Rate each item on a scale from 0 ("not true at all") to 4 ("true nearly all the time").

### Items

| No. | Item (English) | Item (Chinese) |
|:---:|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | I am able to adapt when changes occur. | 当事情发生变化时，我能够适应。 |
| 2 | I can deal with whatever comes my way. | 无论人生路途中发生任何事情，我都能处理它。 |
| 3 | I try to see the humorous side of things when I am faced with problems. | 面临难题时，我试着去看到事情积极的一面。 |
| 4 | Having to cope with stress can make me stronger. | 经历磨炼会让我更有力量。 |
| 5 | I tend to bounce back after illness, injury, or other hardships. | 我很容易从疾病、受伤或困难中恢复过来。 |
| 6 | I believe I can achieve my goals, even if there are obstacles. | 我相信即使遇到障碍我也能够实现我的目标。 |
| 7 | Under pressure, I stay focused and think clearly. | 压力之下，我仍然能够集中精神地思考问题。 |
| 8 | I am not easily discouraged by failure. | 我不会轻易被失败打倒。 |
| 9 | I think of myself as a strong person when dealing with life's challenges and difficulties. | 在处理生活中的挑战和困难时，我觉得我是个坚强的人。 |
| 10 | I am able to handle unpleasant or painful feelings like sadness, fear, and anger. | 我能够处理一些不愉快或痛苦的感觉，例如悲伤、恐惧和愤怒。 |

### Scoring

- **Response options:** 0 = Not true at all, 1 = Rarely true, 2 = Sometimes true, 3 = Often true, 4 = True nearly all the time
- **Score range:** 0-40
- **Scoring method:** Sum all 10 items. There are **no reverse-scored items**.
- **Interpretation:** Higher scores indicate greater resilience. The CD-RISC-10 is unidimensional (single factor).

### Psychometric Properties

| Property | Value | Source |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) | 0.75-0.91 | Multiple studies |
| Test-retest reliability (2 weeks) | r = 0.90 | Earthquake survivors |
| Factor structure | Unidimensional | Campbell-Sills & Stein (2007) |

### Construct Validity

- Negative correlation with depression and PTSD symptoms
- Positive correlation with self-efficacy and social support
- Discriminates between individuals with and without PTSD

## Correspondence Between CD-RISC-25 and CD-RISC-10

The 10 items of the CD-RISC-10 were derived from the original 25-item pool through exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis. The CD-RISC-10 items correspond to the following original CD-RISC-25 item numbers (Campbell-Sills & Stein, 2007):

## Chinese Version Notes

The Chinese version of the CD-RISC was validated by Yu and Zhang (2007), who confirmed the scale's reliability and validity in Chinese populations. The 10-item Chinese version (listed above) has been widely used in Chinese research on resilience.
