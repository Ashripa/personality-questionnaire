## Scale Structure

### 16 Primary Factor Scales

Each factor is bipolar -- both poles carry meaningful personality descriptions.

| Factor Code | Factor Name | Low Pole | High Pole | Items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Warmth | Reserved, impersonal | Warm, attentive to others | 10 |
| B | Reasoning | Concrete thinking | Abstract thinking | 15 |
| C | Emotional Stability | Reactive, emotionally changeable | Emotionally stable, adaptive | 10 |
| E | Dominance | Deferential, cooperative | Dominant, forceful | 10 |
| F | Liveliness | Serious, restrained | Lively, animated | 10 |
| G | Rule-Consciousness | Expedient, nonconforming | Rule-conscious, dutiful | 10 |
| H | Social Boldness | Shy, threat-sensitive | Socially bold, venturesome | 10 |
| I | Sensitivity | Utilitarian, objective | Sensitive, aesthetic | 10 |
| L | Vigilance | Trusting, unsuspecting | Vigilant, suspicious | 10 |
| M | Abstractedness | Grounded, practical | Abstracted, imaginative | 10 |
| N | Privateness | Forthright, genuine | Private, discreet | 10 |
| O | Apprehension | Self-assured, unworried | Apprehensive, self-doubting | 10 |
| Q1 | Openness to Change | Traditional, attached to familiar | Open to change, experimenting | 10 |
| Q2 | Self-Reliance | Group-oriented, affiliative | Self-reliant, solitary | 10 |
| Q3 | Perfectionism | Tolerates disorder, flexible | Perfectionistic, organized | 10 |
| Q4 | Tension | Relaxed, placid | Tense, high energy, driven | 10 |

> **Note**: Factor B (Reasoning) is a brief measure of abstract problem-solving ability, a cognitive factor embedded among the 15 temperament/style scales.

### 5 Global (Second-Order) Factors

The 16 primary factors correlate and can be further factored into five broader dimensions:

| Global Factor | Contributing Primary Factors | Correspondence to Big Five |
|---|---|---|
| Extraversion | A+, F+, H+, N-, Q2- | Extraversion |
| Anxiety | C-, L+, O+, Q4+ | Neuroticism (inverse) |
| Tough-Mindedness | A-, I-, M-, Q1- | Openness (inverse) |
| Independence | E+, H+, L+, Q1+ | -- |
| Self-Control | F-, G+, M-, Q3+ | Conscientiousness |

## Scoring

- Raw scores converted to **Sten scores** (Standard Ten: M = 5.5, SD = 2, range 1--10).
- Sten 1--3: Low range; 4--7: Average range; 8--10: High range.
- Age- and sex-based norms available.
- Response style indices: Impression Management (IM), Infrequency (INF), Acquiescence (ACQ).

---

## Psychometric Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Internal Consistency (alpha, primary scales) | .64 -- .85 (median ~ .74) |
| Internal Consistency (global factors) | .76 -- .91 |
| Test-Retest (2-week) | .69 -- .87 |
| Test-Retest (2-month) | .56 -- .79 |
| Normative Sample | 10,261 adults (U.S.) |

- Factor structure replicated across cultures (40+ countries).
- Convergent validity established with NEO-PI-R, MBTI, CPI, and other personality measures.
- Predictive validity demonstrated in occupational selection, clinical assessment, and educational counseling.

## Sample Items (from published sources)

The 16PF uses situation-based items rather than direct self-assessment statements. Examples from published review articles:

1. "When I find myself in a boring situation, I usually 'tune out' and daydream about other things." (a) True / (b) Uncertain / (c) False
2. "When a bit of tact and convincing is needed to get people moving, I'm usually the one who does it." (a) True / (b) Uncertain / (c) False
3. "I would rather work on a project: (a) with a team of people / (b) uncertain / (c) by myself"

> Note: Actual items are copyrighted by IPAT. These examples are paraphrased from publicly available educational materials.

## Chinese Version

- **Adaptation**: The 16PF was adapted for Chinese populations by Dai Zhongheng and colleagues (Chinese revision of the Cattell 16PF). This is one of the most widely used personality instruments in Chinese applied psychology.
- **Norms**: Chinese norms were developed based on large-scale national samples.
- **Usage**: The Chinese 16PF is extensively used in Chinese occupational selection, military screening, educational counseling, and clinical assessment.
- **Editions**: Multiple Chinese editions exist; the 5th edition adaptation aligned with the international 16PF5 is available through authorized Chinese distributors.
- **Reference**: Dai, Z. H., & Baili, Z. (1988). *Revision of the Chinese norm of Cattell 16PF*. Acta Psychologica Sinica.
- **Limitation**: Some primary factors (especially B - Reasoning) show variable psychometric properties across Chinese samples, and cultural considerations apply to interpretation.
