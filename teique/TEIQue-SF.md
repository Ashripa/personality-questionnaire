## Instructions

> Please answer each statement below by putting a circle around the number that best reflects your degree of agreement or disagreement with that statement. Do not think too long about the exact meaning of the statements. Work quickly and try to answer as accurately as possible. There are no right or wrong answers. There are seven possible responses to each statement ranging from 'Completely Disagree' (number 1) to 'Completely Agree' (number 7).

## Response Scale (7-point Likert)

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completely Disagree | | | | | | Completely Agree |

## Scoring

### Reverse-Scored Items (15 items)

2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 22, 25, 26, 28

### Score Computation

| Score | Formula | Range |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Global Trait EI | Mean of all 30 items | 1.00–7.00 |
| Well-Being | Mean of items 5R, 9, 12R, 20, 24, 27 | 1.00–7.00 |
| Self-Control | Mean of items 4R, 7R, 15, 19, 22R, 30 | 1.00–7.00 |
| Emotionality | Mean of items 1, 2R, 8R, 13R, 16R, 17, 23, 28R | 1.00–7.00 |
| Sociability | Mean of items 6, 10R, 11, 21, 25R, 26R | 1.00–7.00 |

> 4 items (3, 14R, 18R, 29) contribute to Global but not to any factor.

### Psychometric Properties

- Global α = .88 (Chinese sample, Feher et al., 2019)
- Factor α: .47–.82 depending on factor and sample
- One-factor CFA: RMSEA = .096, CFI = .980, SRMR = .024
- Administration time: ~5 minutes
