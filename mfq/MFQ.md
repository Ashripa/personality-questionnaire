# MFQ - Moral Foundations Questionnaire (MFQ-30)

## Citation

Graham, J., Nosek, B. A., Haidt, J., Iyer, R., Koleva, S., & Ditto, P. H. (2011). Mapping the moral domain. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 101(2), 366-385.

**Copyright**: Open research instrument, free for research use. All materials at moralfoundations.org/questionnaires.

---

## Structure

- **32 items total**: 30 scored + 2 catch/attention-check items
- **Part 1** (Moral Relevance): 16 items (15 scored + 1 catch)
- **Part 2** (Moral Judgments): 16 items (15 scored + 1 catch)
- **5 foundations**, each with 6 scored items (3 per part)

## Response Scales (0-5)

**Part 1 — Relevance:**

| Score | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 0 | Not at all relevant |
| 1 | Not very relevant |
| 2 | Slightly relevant |
| 3 | Somewhat relevant |
| 4 | Very relevant |
| 5 | Extremely relevant |

**Part 2 — Judgments:**

| Score | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 0 | Strongly disagree |
| 1 | Moderately disagree |
| 2 | Slightly disagree |
| 3 | Slightly agree |
| 4 | Moderately agree |
| 5 | Strongly agree |

---

## Instructions

**Part 1:**
> When you decide whether something is right or wrong, to what extent are the following considerations relevant to your thinking?

**Part 2:**
> Please read the following sentences and indicate your agreement or disagreement.

## Scoring

1. **Exclude** catch items 6 and 22
2. **Average** (or sum) each foundation's 6 items
3. No reverse scoring needed
4. Average range: 0–5 per foundation; Sum range: 0–30

### Catch Item Criteria

- Item 6: flag if rated ≥ 3 ("somewhat relevant" or higher)
- Item 22: flag if rated ≤ 2 ("slightly disagree" or lower)

### Benchmark (moderate Americans, sum 0-30)

| Foundation | Mean |
|------------|------|
| Harm/Care | 20.2 |
| Fairness/Reciprocity | 20.5 |
| Ingroup/Loyalty | 16.0 |
| Authority/Respect | 16.5 |
| Purity/Sanctity | 12.6 |

### Psychometric Properties

- Cronbach's α: .65–.84 per foundation across studies
- Liberals score higher on Harm & Fairness; Conservatives more balanced across all five
