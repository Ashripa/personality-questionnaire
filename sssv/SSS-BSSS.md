# 第一部分：BSSS — 简版感觉寻求量表

## Part I: Brief Sensation Seeking Scale (BSSS-8)

### 概述

BSSS 由 Hoyle 等人 (2002) 开发，是 Zuckerman SSS-V 的 8 题简版，保留了原量表的四个分量表结构，每个分量表各含 2 个条目。适用于大规模调查和筛查研究。

### 指导语 / Instructions

> 请阅读以下每一条陈述，并根据您的同意程度在 1–5 之间选择一个数字。
>
> Please read each of the following statements and indicate your level of agreement using the scale below.

### 计分方式 / Response Scale

| 分值 | 英文标签 | 中文标签 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Strongly Disagree | 非常不同意 |
| 2 | Disagree | 不同意 |
| 3 | Neither Agree nor Disagree | 既不同意也不反对 |
| 4 | Agree | 同意 |
| 5 | Strongly Agree | 非常同意 |

### 量表条目 / Scale Items

| # | 分量表 | 英文条目 |
|---|--------|----------|
| 1 | ES（经验寻求） | I would like to explore strange places. |
| 2 | ES（经验寻求） | I would like to take off on a trip with no pre-planned routes or timetables. |
| 3 | TAS（刺激与冒险寻求） | I like to do frightening things. |
| 4 | TAS（刺激与冒险寻求） | I would like to try bungee jumping. |
| 5 | DIS（去抑制） | I like wild parties. |
| 6 | DIS（去抑制） | I would love to have new and exciting experiences, even if they are illegal. |
| 7 | BS（对单调的敏感性） | I get restless when I spend too much time at home. |
| 8 | BS（对单调的敏感性） | I prefer friends who are excitingly unpredictable. |

> **注意**：部分验证研究中条目措辞略有变化。例如，条目 4 在某些版本中为 "I would like to try parachute jumping"，条目 6 在某些版本中为 "I like new and exciting experiences, even if I have to break the rules"。上表为多数验证研究中引用最广泛的版本。

### 分量表结构 / Subscale Structure

| 分量表代码 | 英文名称 | 中文名称 | 条目编号 |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| ES | Experience Seeking | 经验寻求 | 1, 2 |
| TAS | Thrill and Adventure Seeking | 刺激与冒险寻求 | 3, 4 |
| DIS | Disinhibition | 去抑制 | 5, 6 |
| BS | Boredom Susceptibility | 对单调的敏感性 | 7, 8 |

### 计分说明 / Scoring Instructions

1. **分量表分数**：将该分量表所含 2 个条目的得分相加（范围 2–10）。
2. **总分**：将全部 8 个条目得分相加（范围 8–40）。
3. **无反向计分题**：所有条目均为正向计分。
4. **解释**：分数越高，感觉寻求倾向越强。

### 心理测量学指标

- Cronbach's α = 0.76（原始研究，Hoyle et al., 2002）
- 多个验证研究报告 α = 0.74–0.81
- 支持单因素结构（总分）和四因素结构（分量表）

# 第二部分：SSS-V — 感觉寻求量表第五版

## Part II: Sensation Seeking Scale, Form V (SSS-V)

### 概述

SSS-V 由 Zuckerman, Eysenck 与 Eysenck (1978) 在跨文化因素分析基础上开发，是感觉寻求领域引用最广泛的自评量表。包含 40 个迫选题目，分为四个分量表，每个分量表各含 10 题。

### 指导语 / Instructions

> 以下每一题包含两个陈述（A 和 B），请选择更符合您的感受或偏好的一个选项。即使两个选项都不完全符合，也请选择相对更接近的一项。
>
> Each of the items below contains two choices, A and B. Please indicate which of the choices most describes your likes or the way you feel. In some cases you may find items in which both choices describe your likes or feelings. Please choose the one which better describes your likes or feelings. In some cases you may find items in which you do not like either choice. In these cases mark the choice you dislike least.

### 分量表结构 / Subscale Structure

| 分量表代码 | 英文名称 | 中文名称 | 条目编号 | 描述 |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|------|
| TAS | Thrill and Adventure Seeking | 刺激与冒险寻求 | 3, 11, 16, 17, 20, 21, 23, 28, 38, 40 | 渴望参与户外运动和具有危险性的身体活动 |
| ES | Experience Seeking | 经验寻求 | 4, 6, 9, 10, 14, 18, 19, 22, 26, 37 | 通过思想、感觉、旅行和非传统生活方式寻求新异体验 |
| DIS | Disinhibition | 去抑制 | 1, 12, 13, 25, 29, 30, 32, 33, 35, 36 | 通过聚会、饮酒和性多样性等社会活动寻求释放 |
| BS | Boredom Susceptibility | 对单调的敏感性 | 2, 5, 7, 8, 15, 24, 27, 31, 34, 39 | 对重复、单调和令人乏味的环境极度厌烦 |

### 全部 40 个条目 / Complete 40 Items

> **说明**：每题含 A、B 两个选项，被试选择更符合自己的一项。标记为 **★** 的选项为计分键（sensation-seeking keyed response），选择该选项计 1 分。

---

#### 去抑制分量表 (DIS) — Disinhibition

**第 1 题**
- **★ A.** I like "wild" uninhibited parties.
- B. I prefer quiet parties with good conversation.

**第 12 题**
- A. I dislike "swingers" (people who are free with sex).
- **★ B.** I enjoy the company of real "swingers."

**第 13 题**
- A. I find that stimulants make me uncomfortable.
- **★ B.** I often like to get high (drinking liquor or smoking marijuana).

**第 25 题**
- A. I am not interested in experience for its own sake.
- **★ B.** I like to have new and exciting experiences and sensations even if they are a little frightening or unconventional.

**第 29 题**
- **★ A.** I like to date members of the opposite sex who are physically exciting.
- B. I like to date members of the opposite sex who share my values.

**第 30 题**
- A. Heavy drinking usually ruins a party because some people get loud and boisterous.
- **★ B.** Keeping the drinks full is the key to a good party.

**第 32 题**
- **★ A.** A person should have considerable sexual experience before marriage.
- B. It's better if two married persons begin their sexual experience with each other.

**第 33 题**
- A. Even if I had the money I would not care to associate with flighty rich persons.
- **★ B.** I could conceive of myself seeking pleasures around the world with the "jet set."

**第 35 题**
- A. There is altogether too much portrayal of sex in movies.
- **★ B.** I enjoy watching many of the "sexy" scenes in movies.

**第 36 题**
- **★ A.** I feel best after taking a couple of drinks.
- B. Something is wrong with people who need liquor to feel good.

---

#### 对单调的敏感性分量表 (BS) — Boredom Susceptibility

**第 2 题**
- A. There are some movies I enjoy seeing a second or even a third time.
- **★ B.** I can't stand watching a movie that I've seen before.

**第 5 题**
- **★ A.** I get bored seeing the same old faces.
- B. I like the comfortable familiarity of everyday friends.

**第 7 题**
- A. I dislike people who do or say things just to shock or upset others.
- **★ B.** When you can predict almost everything a person will do and say he or she must be a bore.

**第 8 题**
- **★ A.** I usually don't enjoy a movie or play where I can predict what will happen in advance.
- B. I don't mind watching a movie or play where I can predict what will happen in advance.

**第 15 题**
- A. I enjoy looking at home movies or travel slides.
- **★ B.** Looking at someone's home movies or travel slides bores me tremendously.

**第 24 题**
- **★ A.** I prefer friends who are excitingly unpredictable.
- B. I prefer friends who are reliable and predictable.

**第 27 题**
- A. I enjoy spending time in the familiar surroundings of home.
- **★ B.** I get restless if I have to stay around home for any length of time.

**第 31 题**
- A. The worst social sin is to be rude.
- **★ B.** The worst social sin is to be a bore.

**第 34 题**
- **★ A.** I like people who are sharp and witty even if they do sometimes insult others.
- B. I dislike people who have their fun at the expense of hurting the feelings of others.

**第 39 题**
- **★ A.** I have no patience with dull or boring persons.
- B. I find something interesting in almost every person I talk to.

---

#### 刺激与冒险寻求分量表 (TAS) — Thrill and Adventure Seeking

**第 3 题**
- **★ A.** I often wish I could be a mountain climber.
- B. I can't understand people who risk their necks climbing mountains.

**第 11 题**
- A. A sensible person avoids activities that are dangerous.
- **★ B.** I sometimes like to do things that are a little frightening.

**第 16 题**
- **★ A.** I would like to take up the sport of water skiing.
- B. I would not like to take up water skiing.

**第 17 题**
- **★ A.** I would like to try surfboard riding.
- B. I would not like to try surfboard riding.

**第 20 题**
- A. I would not like to learn to fly an airplane.
- **★ B.** I would like to learn to fly an airplane.

**第 21 题**
- A. I prefer the surface of the water to the depths.
- **★ B.** I would like to go scuba diving.

**第 23 题**
- **★ A.** I would like to try parachute jumping.
- B. I would never want to try jumping out of a plane, with or without a parachute.

**第 28 题**
- **★ A.** I like to dive off the high board.
- B. I don't like the feeling I get standing on the high board (or I don't go near it at all).

**第 38 题**
- A. Sailing long distances in small sailing crafts is foolhardy.
- **★ B.** I would like to sail a long distance in a small but seaworthy sailing craft.

**第 40 题**
- A. Skiing down a high mountain slope is a good way to end up on crutches.
- **★ B.** I think I would enjoy the sensations of skiing very fast down a high mountain slope.

---

#### 经验寻求分量表 (ES) — Experience Seeking

**第 4 题**
- A. I dislike all body odors.
- **★ B.** I like some of the earthy body smells.

**第 6 题**
- **★ A.** I like to explore a strange city or section of town by myself, even if it means getting lost.
- B. I prefer a guide when I am in a place I don't know well.

**第 9 题**
- **★ A.** I have tried marijuana or would like to.
- B. I would never smoke marijuana.

**第 10 题**
- A. I would not like to try any drug which might produce strange and dangerous effects on me.
- **★ B.** I would like to try some of the new drugs that produce hallucinations.

**第 14 题**
- **★ A.** I like to try new foods that I have never tasted before.
- B. I order the dishes with which I am familiar, so as to avoid disappointment and unpleasantness.

**第 18 题**
- **★ A.** I would like to take off on a trip with no preplanned or definite routes, or timetables.
- B. When I go on a trip I like to plan my route and timetable fairly carefully.

**第 19 题**
- A. I prefer the "down to earth" kinds of people as friends.
- **★ B.** I would like to make friends in some of the "far-out" groups like artists or "punks."

**第 22 题**
- **★ A.** I would like to meet some persons who are homosexual (men or women).
- B. I stay away from anyone I suspect of being "gay" or "lesbian."

**第 26 题**
- A. The essence of good art is in its clarity, symmetry of form, and harmony of colors.
- **★ B.** I often find beauty in the "clashing" colors and irregular forms of modern paintings.

**第 37 题**
- A. People should dress according to some standard of taste, neatness, and style.
- **★ B.** People should dress in individual ways even if the effects are sometimes strange.

# 第三部分：中文版本

## Part III: Chinese Versions

### SSS-V 中文版

SSS-V 中文版由北京大学心理学系**陈仲庚**与**张雨青**于 1988 年翻译并修订，发表于《心理学报》。该量表保留了原始 40 题迫选格式和四个分量表结构。

- **信度**：各分量表信度系数为 0.68–0.81
- **效度**：达到可接受水平
- **引用**：陈仲庚, 张雨青. (1988). 感觉寻求的人格特质及在临床上的应用. *心理学报, 20*(3), 106–112.

**四个分量表中文名称**：

| 英文 | 中文（大陆） | 中文（台湾） |
|------|------------|------------|
| Thrill and Adventure Seeking (TAS) | 刺激性和冒险性寻求 | 刺激與冒險尋求 |
| Experience Seeking (ES) | 经验寻求 | 經驗尋求 |
| Disinhibition (DIS) | 去抑制 | 去抑制 |
| Boredom Susceptibility (BS) | 对单调的敏感性 | 對單調的敏感性 |

> **注意**：SSS-V 中文版的完整 40 题中文条目需参阅原始文献（陈仲庚 & 张雨青, 1988）或联系原作者获取。由于版权限制，此处不列出完整中文条目。

### BSSS 中文版

#### BSSS-C（大陆版）

Chen 等人 (2013) 为中国大陆文化背景改编了 BSSS，保留了 8 题 4 分量表结构，但对个别条目进行了文化适应性修改，使其在非西方文化中更具适用性。

- **Cronbach's α** = 0.90
- **与原版 BSSS 相关** r = 0.85 (p < .01)
- **验证性因素分析**支持四因素模型
- **效标效度**：能有效预测饮酒、吸烟和性风险行为意向

**引用**：Chen, X., Li, F., Nydegger, L., Gong, J., Ren, Y., Dinaj-Koci, V., Sun, H., & Stanton, B. (2013). Brief Sensation Seeking Scale for Chinese — Cultural adaptation and psychometric assessment. *Personality and Individual Differences, 54*(5), 604–609.

#### 台湾版简易版 SSS-V

林素真 (2012) 从 SSS-V 中文版中筛选出 8 题构建简易版，采用跨国因素分析结果确保条目的跨文化适用性。

- **因素载荷**：0.45–0.93
- **组合信度**：TAS = .75, ES = .60, DIS = .61, BS = .57
- **收敛效度**：TAS = .61, ES = .43, DIS = .45, BS = .40

**引用**：林素真. (2012). 簡易版刺激尋求量表的發展及驗證. *健康促進與衛生教育學報, 37*, 23–53.

#### 大陆 BSSS 维度结构

Fung (2020) 在大陆大学生样本中发现，BSSS-8 的单因素结构（vs. 四因素）更为适用。

**引用**：Fung, S. (2020). Revisiting the dimensionality of the Brief Sensation Seeking Scale in Mainland China. *Social Behavior and Personality, 48*(2), e8619.
