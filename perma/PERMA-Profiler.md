# PERMA-Profiler

## Citation

Butler, J., & Kern, M. L. (2016). The PERMA-Profiler: A brief multidimensional measure of flourishing. *International Journal of Wellbeing*, *6*(3), 1--48. https://doi.org/10.5502/ijw.v6i3.526

---

## Construct

The PERMA-Profiler operationalizes Seligman's (2011) well-being theory, which defines flourishing through five pillars:

| Pillar | Full Name | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **P** | Positive Emotion | Feeling joyful, contented, and positive |
| **E** | Engagement | Being absorbed, interested, and in flow |
| **R** | Relationships | Feeling loved, supported, and socially connected |
| **M** | Meaning | Leading a purposeful and valuable life |
| **A** | Accomplishment | Making progress and achieving goals |

The Profiler also includes filler/supplementary items measuring:

| Code | Domain | Items |
|------|--------|:-----:|
| **N** | Negative Emotion | 3 |
| **H** | Physical Health | 3 |
| **Lon** | Loneliness | 1 |
| **Hap** | Overall Happiness | 1 |

---

## Instructions

> We are interested in how you feel about different aspects of your life. Please answer each question on a scale from 0 to 10.

(Note: No time frame is specified; items capture general tendencies.)

---

## Response Scale

All items use an **11-point scale (0--10)**, but the verbal anchors differ by item group:

| Anchor Set | Items | 0 | 5 | 10 |
|------------|-------|---|---|-----|
| Frequency | P1, P2, E1, E3, A1, A2, A3, N1, N2, N3 | Never | -- | Always |
| Extent | P3, E2, R1, R2, M1, M2, M3 | Not at all | -- | Completely |
| Satisfaction | R3, H2 | Not at all | -- | Completely |
| Quality | H1, H3 | Terrible | -- | Excellent |
| Degree | Lon, Hap | Not at all | -- | Completely |

## Reverse Scoring

**No items are reverse-scored.**

Negative Emotion (N) and Loneliness (Lon) are scored in their original direction (higher = more negative emotion / more loneliness) and reported separately. They are **not** subtracted from the overall well-being score.

---

## Scoring

### Domain Scores

Each domain score = **mean of its 3 items** (range 0--10):

| Domain | Items | Range |
|--------|-------|:-----:|
| Positive Emotion (P) | P1, P2, P3 | 0--10 |
| Engagement (E) | E1, E2, E3 | 0--10 |
| Relationships (R) | R1, R2, R3 | 0--10 |
| Meaning (M) | M1, M2, M3 | 0--10 |
| Accomplishment (A) | A1, A2, A3 | 0--10 |
| Negative Emotion (N) | N1, N2, N3 | 0--10 |
| Physical Health (H) | H1, H2, H3 | 0--10 |

### Overall Well-Being Score

**Overall Well-Being** = Mean of the 15 PERMA items (P1--P3, E1--E3, R1--R3, M1--M3, A1--A3) **+ Hap** (16 items total, range 0--10).

- Negative Emotion, Physical Health, and Loneliness are reported as separate indicators and are **not** included in the overall well-being composite.

## Chinese Version (中文版)

A Chinese adaptation of the PERMA-Profiler exists and has been used in research. One version uses a 7-point Likert scale (1 = "完全不符合" to 7 = "完全符合") rather than the original 0--10 scale. The nine dimensions are rendered as:

| English | Chinese |
|---------|---------|
| Positive Emotion | 积极情绪 |
| Engagement | 投入 |
| Relationships | 人际关系 |
| Meaning | 意义 |
| Accomplishment | 成就 |
| Overall Happiness | 总体幸福感 |
| Negative Emotion | 消极情绪 |
| Loneliness | 孤独感 |
| Physical Health | 身体健康 |

Note: The modified 7-point Likert response format differs from the original 0--10 scale. Researchers using the Chinese version should verify which response format was employed and consult the specific validation study. The overall Cronbach's alpha for the Chinese version has been reported as .90, with a KMO value of .918.

## Psychometric Properties

### Internal Consistency (Cronbach's Alpha)

Combined samples, *N* = 31,966 (Butler & Kern, 2016):

| Domain | Alpha |
|--------|:-----:|
| Positive Emotion (P) | .88 |
| Engagement (E) | .72 |
| Relationships (R) | .82 |
| Meaning (M) | .90 |
| Accomplishment (A) | .79 |
| Overall Well-Being | .94 |
| Negative Emotion (N) | .71 |
| Physical Health (H) | .92 |

### Confirmatory Factor Analysis

| Index | Value |
|-------|:-----:|
| RMSEA | .064 |
| SRMR | .031 |
| CFI | .967 |
| TLI | .956 |

The five-factor PERMA model (with correlated factors) showed good fit. A bifactor model with a general well-being factor also fit well.

### Test-Retest Reliability

Test-retest correlations ranged from *r* = .51 to .90 across domains (varying intervals and samples).

### Convergent Validity

- PERMA domains correlate as expected with established measures of subjective well-being (e.g., Satisfaction with Life Scale, Flourishing Scale).
- Overall well-being correlates strongly with the Satisfaction with Life Scale (*r* > .60).

### Discriminant Validity

- PERMA domains are distinguishable from each other (inter-domain correlations moderate, typically *r* = .40--.70).
- Negative Emotion correlates negatively with PERMA domains but is a distinct construct.

### Norms

Population norms are available in Appendix 3 of Butler & Kern (2016), broken down by gender, age group, and country.
