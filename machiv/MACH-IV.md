# MACH-IV - Machiavellianism Scale

## Instructions

> Rate each statement on the scale below indicating the degree to which you agree or disagree.

## Response Scale (7-point Likert)

| Score | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | Strongly disagree |
| 2 | Moderately disagree |
| 3 | Slightly disagree |
| 4 | Neutral (no opinion) |
| 5 | Slightly agree |
| 6 | Moderately agree |
| 7 | Strongly agree |

> 注: 部分中文版使用5点量表（非常不同意→非常同意），分数范围 20-100。

## Scoring

1. **Reverse-score** items 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 14, 16, 17: 7点量表用 (8 − 原始分)
2. **Sum** all 20 items (range: 20–140)
3. **Add constant 20** → final range: **40–160**, theoretical midpoint = **100**
4. Scores > 100 = "High Mach"; < 100 = "Low Mach"

### Three Content Subscales

| Subscale | Content |
|----------|---------|
| Interpersonal Tactics | Manipulative strategies |
| Views of Human Nature | Cynical beliefs about people |
| Abstract Morality | Views on ethical standards |

> 通常仅使用总分，不分维度计分。

### Psychometric Properties

- Cronbach's α: .65–.76 (modest, due to multidimensional structure)
- Test-retest: .64–.87 depending on interval
- Used in 500+ published studies

### Known Limitations

- Items 14, 18, 20 use gendered language ("men")
- Item 17 references "Barnum" (culturally dated)
- Dahling et al. (2009) developed a separate 16-item Machiavellian Personality Scale (MPS), not a revision of MACH-IV
