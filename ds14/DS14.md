# DS14: Type D Personality Scale

## Citation

Denollet, J. (2005). DS14: Standard assessment of negative affectivity, social inhibition, and Type D personality. *Psychosomatic Medicine*, *67*(1), 89-97. https://doi.org/10.1097/01.psy.0000149256.81953.49

Chinese adaptation:

于肖楠, 张建新. (2006). D型人格量表(DS14)在中国两所大学生样本中的试用. *中国心理卫生杂志*, *20*(5), 313-316.

## Overview

The DS14 is a 14-item self-report questionnaire designed to assess Type D (Distressed) personality, defined as the joint tendency toward **Negative Affectivity (NA)** and **Social Inhibition (SI)**. Type D personality has been established as a predictor of adverse clinical events and poorer prognosis in patients with cardiovascular disease.

## Instructions

> Below are a number of statements that people often use to describe themselves. Please read each statement and then circle the appropriate number next to that statement to indicate your answer. There are no right or wrong answers: Your own impression is the only thing that matters.

## Items (English and Chinese)

| # | Subscale | English | Chinese | Reverse-scored |
|---|----------|---------|---------|----------------|
| 1 | SI | I make contact easily when I meet people | 我善于与人打交道 | **Yes** |
| 2 | NA | I often make a fuss about unimportant things | 我常常为一些琐事而小题大做 | No |
| 3 | SI | I often talk to strangers | 我常常与陌生人交谈 | **Yes** |
| 4 | NA | I often feel unhappy | 我经常感到不开心 | No |
| 5 | NA | I am often irritated | 我经常感到烦躁不安 | No |
| 6 | SI | I often feel inhibited in social interactions | 与人交往时，我常常感到很拘谨 | No |
| 7 | NA | I take a gloomy view of things | 我对待事物的态度是悲观的 | No |
| 8 | SI | I find it hard to start a conversation | 我觉得很难打开话题 | No |
| 9 | NA | I am often in a bad mood | 我常常心情不好 | No |
| 10 | SI | I am a closed kind of person | 我是一个自我封闭的人 | No |
| 11 | SI | I would rather keep other people at a distance | 我较喜欢与人保持距离 | No |
| 12 | NA | I often find myself worrying about something | 我常常忧心忡忡 | No |

## Reverse Scoring

Items **1** and **3** are reverse-scored. For these items, recode as follows:

| Original | Recoded |
|----------|---------|
| 0 | 4 |
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 0 |

## Scoring Method

1. Reverse-score items 1 and 3.
2. **Negative Affectivity (NA)** subscale: Sum items 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 12, 13. Range: 0--28.
3. **Social Inhibition (SI)** subscale: Sum items 1 (recoded), 3 (recoded), 6, 8, 10, 11, 14. Range: 0--28.
4. **Type D classification**: A participant is classified as Type D if they score **>= 10 on both NA and SI** simultaneously.
5. NA and SI can also be used as continuous variables for dimensional analyses.

## Psychometric Properties

### Original Validation (Denollet, 2005; N = 3,678)

| Property | NA | SI |
|----------|----|----|
| Cronbach's alpha | 0.88 | 0.86 |
| 3-month test-retest *r* | 0.72 | 0.82 |
| Factor loadings | 0.62--0.82 | 0.62--0.82 |

- Confirmatory factor analysis supported a stable two-factor structure (NA and SI).
- The two subscales showed adequate convergent and divergent validity with established personality measures (e.g., NEO-FFI Neuroticism correlates with NA; NEO-FFI Extraversion inversely correlates with SI).

### Chinese Version (Yu & Zhang, 2006)

| Property | NA | SI |
|----------|----|----|
| Cronbach's alpha | 0.90 | 0.80 |

- The two-factor structure was replicated in Chinese college student samples.
- Chinese cutoff criteria reported in some studies: NA >= 6 and SI >= 9 (note: the standard international cutoff remains >= 10 on both).
