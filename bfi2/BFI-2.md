## Overview

The BFI-2 is a 60-item self-report inventory measuring the Big Five personality domains, each with 3 facets (15 facets total). It uses short, descriptive phrases with the common stem "I am someone who..."

**Structure:**

| Domain | Facet 1 | Facet 2 | Facet 3 |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Extraversion (E) | Sociability | Assertiveness | Energy Level |
| Agreeableness (A) | Compassion | Respectfulness | Trust |
| Conscientiousness (C) | Organization | Productiveness | Responsibility |
| Negative Emotionality (N) | Anxiety | Depression | Emotional Volatility |
| Open-Mindedness (O) | Intellectual Curiosity | Aesthetic Sensitivity | Creative Imagination |

---

## Instructions / 指导语

**English:**

> Here are a number of characteristics that may or may not apply to you. For example, do you agree that you are someone who *likes to spend time with others*? Please write a number next to each statement to indicate the extent to which you agree or disagree with that statement.

**Chinese / 中文:**

> 下面是一些关于个人特征的描述，有些可能适用于你，有些可能不适用于你。比如，你是否同意"你是一个喜欢与他人待在一起的人"？请在下面每个句子前的横线上填入对应的数字以表明你同意或不同意这个描述。

## Response Scale / 作答量表

| Score | English | Chinese |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Disagree strongly | 非常不同意 |
| 2 | Disagree a little | 不太同意 |
| 3 | Neutral; no opinion | 态度中立 |
| 4 | Agree a little | 比较同意 |
| 5 | Agree strongly | 非常同意 |

**Stem / 题干:** "I am someone who..." / "我是一个......的人"

## Psychometric Properties Summary

### BFI-2 (60 items)
- Domain alpha reliability: .83 - .88 (Internet sample); .85+ (student sample)
- Facet alpha reliability: .66 - .85 (mean = .76-.77)
- Test-retest reliability (domain): .78 - .87
- Administration time: approximately 5-10 minutes

### BFI-2-S (30 items)
- Domain alpha reliability: .74 - .83
- Domain-level convergence with full BFI-2: r = .94 - .97
- Can be used for facet assessment in large samples

### BFI-2-XS (15 items)
- Domain alpha reliability: .51 - .68
- Captures ~80% of the full BFI-2 variance at domain level
- Should only be used when brevity is essential and domain-level assessment suffices

## Comparison of Three Forms

| Feature | BFI-2 | BFI-2-S | BFI-2-XS |
|---------|-------|---------|----------|
| Number of items | 60 | 30 | 15 |
| Items per domain | 12 | 6 | 3 |
| Items per facet | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Domain assessment | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Facet assessment | Yes | Yes (large N) | No |
| Administration time | 5-10 min | 3-5 min | 1-2 min |
