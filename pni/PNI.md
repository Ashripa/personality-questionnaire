# Pathological Narcissism Inventory (PNI)

## Citation

Pincus, A. L., Ansell, E. B., Pimentel, C. A., Cain, N. M., Wright, A. G. C., & Levy, K. N. (2009). Initial construction and validation of the Pathological Narcissism Inventory. *Psychological Assessment, 21*(3), 365-379. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0016530

## Overview

The PNI is a 52-item self-report measure assessing 7 dimensions of pathological narcissism spanning problems with **narcissistic grandiosity** and **narcissistic vulnerability**. The PNI structure was validated via confirmatory factor analysis (N = 2,801; CFI = .97, SRMR = .052, RMSEA = .049). Completion time is approximately 12 minutes or less.

## Instructions

Below are a number of statements that may or may not apply to you. Please rate each item on a scale from 0 to 5.

**Response Scale:**

| 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not at all like me | | | | | Very much like me |

## Scoring

### Subscale Scoring

Each subscale score is calculated as the **mean** of its constituent items (to ease comparison across scales of different lengths).

| Subscale | Abbreviation | Items | # Items |
|----------|-------------|-------|---------|
| Contingent Self-Esteem | CSE | 2, 5, 8, 16, 19, 30, 32, 36, 40, 41, 47, 48 | 12 |
| Exploitative | EXP | 4, 10, 15, 23, 35 | 5 |
| Self-Sacrificing Self-Enhancement | SSSE | 6, 22, 25, 33, 39, 43 | 6 |
| Hiding the Self | HS | 7, 9, 13, 28, 44, 46, 50 | 7 |
| Grandiose Fantasy | GF | 1, 14, 26, 31, 42, 45, 49 | 7 |
| Devaluing | DEV | 3, 17, 21, 24, 27, 34, 51 | 7 |
| Entitlement Rage | ER | 11, 12, 18, 20, 29, 37, 38, 52 | 8 |

**No items are reverse-scored.**

### Higher-Order Factor Structure

| Higher-Order Factor | Subscales |
|--------------------|-----------|
| **Narcissistic Grandiosity** | EXP, GF, SSSE, ER |
| **Narcissistic Vulnerability** | CSE, HS, DEV |

Note: The assignment of ER to Grandiosity follows Wright, Lukowitsky, & Pincus (2010). Some studies place ER under Vulnerability or treat it as cross-loading. The original 2009 paper notes that the optimal higher-order structure was still under investigation.

Higher-order factor scores = mean of constituent subscale means.

### PNI Total Score

PNI Total = mean of all 52 items. Internal consistency alpha = .95 (N = 2,801).
