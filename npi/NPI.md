# Narcissistic Personality Inventory (NPI)

## Citations

- **NPI-40**: Raskin, R., & Terry, H. (1988). A principal-components analysis of the Narcissistic Personality Inventory and further evidence of its construct validity. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 54(5), 890-902.
- **NPI-16**: Ames, D. R., Rose, P., & Anderson, C. P. (2006). The NPI-16 as a short measure of narcissism. *Journal of Research in Personality*, 40(4), 440-450.
- **Original NPI**: Raskin, R., & Hall, C. S. (1979). A narcissistic personality inventory. *Psychological Reports*, 45(2), 590.

---

## Instructions

The NPI is a forced-choice self-report measure of subclinical (non-pathological) narcissism. For each pair of statements (A and B), choose the one that comes closest to describing your feelings and beliefs about yourself. There are no right or wrong answers.

---

## NPI-16: Short Form

The NPI-16 (Ames, Rose, & Anderson, 2006) is a 16-item subset of the NPI-40 designed for situations where brevity is needed. It yields a single total score (0-16) and does not provide subscale scores. Internal consistency: alpha = .65-.78; test-retest reliability: r = .85 (5-week interval); correlation with NPI-40: r = .90.

|---|---|---|

| 2 | 9 | I think I am a special person. |
| 3 | 23 | Everybody likes to hear my stories. |

| 5 | 12 | I like to have authority over other people. |

| 14 | 4 | I know that I am good because everybody keeps telling me so. |

| 16 | 39 | I am more capable than other people. |

### NPI-16 Scoring

For each of the 16 items, score 1 point if the respondent selects the narcissistic statement, 0 otherwise. Sum all 16 items for a total score (range: 0-16).

---

## NPI-40 Scoring Instructions

1. **Total Score**: Count the number of narcissistic responses (marked N) selected across all 40 items. Range: 0-40. Higher scores indicate greater narcissism.
2. **Subscale Scores**: Count the number of narcissistic responses within each subscale group. Sum the items belonging to each subscale as listed in the Subscale Summary table.
3. **Interpretation**: The NPI measures subclinical narcissism in the general population. It is NOT a diagnostic tool for Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD). There are no established clinical cutoffs. Sample means in college populations typically range from 15-17 (SD ~ 6-7).
