# Brief-COPE (Brief Coping Orientation to Problems Experienced)

## Citation

Carver, C. S. (1997). You want to measure coping but your protocol's too long: Consider the Brief COPE. *International Journal of Behavioral Medicine, 4*(1), 92-100.

Original full COPE:
Carver, C. S., Scheier, M. F., & Weintraub, J. K. (1989). Assessing coping strategies: A theoretically based approach. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 56*(2), 267-283.

---

## Overview

The Brief-COPE is a 28-item self-report questionnaire that measures 14 conceptually differentiable coping strategies. It is an abbreviated version of the original 60-item COPE Inventory (Carver, Scheier, & Weintraub, 1989). Each of the 14 subscales contains 2 items.

---

## Instructions (English)

> The following items ask what you've been doing to cope with a stressful event. There are many ways to try to deal with problems. These items ask what you've been doing to cope with this one. Obviously, different people deal with things in different ways, but I'm interested in how you've tried to deal with it. Each item says something about a particular way of coping. I want to know to what extent you've been doing what the item says. How much or how frequently. Don't answer on the basis of whether it seems to be working or not -- just whether or not you're doing it. Use these response choices. Try to rate each item separately in your mind from the others. Make your answers as true FOR YOU as you can.

## Instructions (Chinese / 中文指导语)

> 以下各条目描述了人们在面对压力事件时可能采取的不同应对方式。请根据您自己的实际情况，按照每个条目所描述的内容，评估您使用该方式的频率。请不要根据它是否有效来回答，只需回答您是否这样做了。

### Subscale Scoring

### Higher-Order Factor Groupings

Some researchers group the 14 subscales into broader coping categories:

**Problem-Focused Coping (问题聚焦应对)**
- Active Coping (Items 2, 7)
- Instrumental Support (Items 10, 23)
- Planning (Items 14, 25)
- Positive Reframing (Items 12, 17)

**Emotion-Focused Coping (情感聚焦应对)**
- Emotional Support (Items 5, 15)
- Venting (Items 9, 21)
- Humor (Items 18, 28)
- Acceptance (Items 20, 24)
- Self-Blame (Items 13, 26)
- Religion (Items 22, 27)

**Avoidant Coping (回避型应对)**
- Self-Distraction (Items 1, 19)
- Denial (Items 3, 8)
- Substance Use (Items 4, 11)
- Behavioral Disengagement (Items 6, 16)

> Note: Carver (1997) recommends examining the 14 subscales separately rather than combining them into higher-order factors, as the factor structure may vary across populations and contexts.

---

## Scoring Notes

- There are **no reverse-scored items**.
- There is **no total score**; only subscale scores are computed.
- Higher subscale scores indicate greater use of that particular coping strategy.
- The scale can be administered in both **dispositional** ("how you usually cope") and **situational** ("how you coped with a specific event") formats by adjusting the instructions.

## Psychometric Properties

### Reliability (Internal Consistency)

Based on Carver (1997) and subsequent validation studies:

| Subscale | Cronbach's alpha |
|----------|-----------------|
| Active Coping | .68 |
| Planning | .73 |
| Positive Reframing | .64 |
| Acceptance | .57 |
| Humor | .73 |
| Religion | .82 |
| Emotional Support | .71 |
| Instrumental Support | .64 |
| Self-Distraction | .71 |
| Denial | .54 |
| Venting | .50 |
| Substance Use | .90 |
| Behavioral Disengagement | .65 |
| Self-Blame | .69 |

> Note: Given that each subscale has only 2 items, Cronbach's alpha values above .50 are generally considered acceptable (Carver, 1997).

### Validity

- The Brief-COPE retains the factor structure of the original COPE inventory.
- Convergent validity has been demonstrated through correlations with personality traits (e.g., optimism positively correlates with active coping; neuroticism positively correlates with avoidant coping).
- The scale has been widely validated across diverse populations including clinical samples, caregivers, students, disaster survivors, and cross-cultural samples.
- The Chinese version has been validated in Chinese nursing students and other Chinese populations, demonstrating adequate psychometric properties.

---

## Administration

- **Time**: Approximately 5-10 minutes.
- **Format**: Self-report; can be administered individually or in groups; paper-and-pencil or online.
- **Population**: Adults (18+); has been used with adolescents in some studies.
- **Languages**: Available in numerous languages including English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Arabic, and many others.
