# PAI — Personality Assessment Inventory

## APA Citation

Morey, L. C. (1991). *Personality Assessment Inventory: Professional manual*. Odessa, FL: Psychological Assessment Resources (PAR).

Morey, L. C. (2007). *Personality Assessment Inventory: Professional manual* (2nd ed.). Lutz, FL: Psychological Assessment Resources (PAR).

---

## Overview

The PAI is a 344-item self-report questionnaire developed using a **construct validation framework** to assess psychopathology, personality, and treatment-relevant variables in adults. It features **22 non-overlapping full scales** (no item belongs to more than one scale), of which 10 contain conceptually derived subscales (31 subscales total).

| Property        | Value                    |
|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Total items     | 344                      |
| Full scales     | 22 (non-overlapping)     |
| Subscales       | 31                       |
| Response format | 4-point Likert           |
| Admin time      | 40–50 min                |
| Reading level   | 4th grade                |
| Age range       | 18+ (PAI-A for 12–18)   |

## Scale Structure

### Validity Scales (4 scales)

| Scale | Abbreviation | Items | Description |
|-------|:------------:|:-----:|-------------|
| Inconsistency | ICN | 10 pairs | Back-to-back similar items; detects random/careless responding |
| Infrequency | INF | 8 | Items with very low endorsement rate in normative sample |
| Negative Impression Management | NIM | 9 | Exaggeration of psychopathology / malingering |
| Positive Impression Management | PIM | 9 | Minimization of problems / social desirability |

### Clinical Scales (11 scales, 24 items each except ALC and DRG)

| Scale | Abbr | Items | Subscales |
|-------|:----:|:-----:|-----------|
| Somatic Complaints | SOM | 24 | SOM-C (Conversion, 8), SOM-S (Somatization, 8), SOM-H (Health Concerns, 8) |
| Anxiety | ANX | 24 | ANX-C (Cognitive, 8), ANX-A (Affective, 8), ANX-P (Physiological, 8) |
| Anxiety-Related Disorders | ARD | 24 | ARD-O (Obsessive-Compulsive, 8), ARD-P (Phobias, 8), ARD-T (Traumatic Stress, 8) |
| Depression | DEP | 24 | DEP-C (Cognitive, 8), DEP-A (Affective, 8), DEP-P (Physiological, 8) |
| Mania | MAN | 24 | MAN-A (Activity Level, 8), MAN-G (Grandiosity, 8), MAN-I (Irritability, 8) |
| Paranoia | PAR | 24 | PAR-H (Hypervigilance, 8), PAR-P (Persecution, 8), PAR-R (Resentment, 8) |
| Schizophrenia | SCZ | 24 | SCZ-P (Psychotic Experiences, 8), SCZ-S (Social Detachment, 8), SCZ-T (Thought Disorder, 8) |
| Borderline Features | BOR | 24 | BOR-A (Affective Instability, 6), BOR-I (Identity Problems, 6), BOR-N (Negative Relationships, 6), BOR-S (Self-Harm, 6) |
| Antisocial Features | ANT | 24 | ANT-A (Antisocial Behaviors, 8), ANT-E (Egocentricity, 8), ANT-S (Stimulus-Seeking, 8) |
| Alcohol Problems | ALC | 12 | *(no subscales)* |

| Scale | Abbr | Items | Subscales |
|-------|:----:|:-----:|-----------|
| Aggression | AGG | 18 | AGG-A (Aggressive Attitude, 6), AGG-V (Verbal Aggression, 6), AGG-P (Physical Aggression, 6) |

| Stress | STR | 8 | *(no subscales)* |
| Nonsupport | NON | 8 | *(no subscales)* |

| Scale | Abbr | Items | Description |

## Response Format

4-point Likert scale: **F** = *False, not at all true*; **ST** = *Slightly true*; **MT** = *Mainly true*; **VT** = *Very true*

---

## Scoring

- Raw scores converted to **T-scores** (M = 50, SD = 10) based on community normative sample (N = 1,000; census-stratified).
- Clinical and college norms also available.
- **Supplemental indices:** Malingering Index (MAL), Rogers Discriminant Function (RDF), Defensiveness Index (DEF), Cashel Discriminant Function (CDF).

---

## Psychometric Properties

- **Internal consistency:** Median alpha = .81 (full scales); range .66–.90 across normative and clinical samples.
- **Test-retest reliability:** Median r = .82 (24-day interval; normative sample); median r = .73 (28-day; clinical sample).
- **Discriminant validity:** Non-overlapping scale design yields lower inter-scale correlations than MMPI-2.
- **Standardization:** Community sample (N = 1,000), clinical sample (N = 1,246), college sample (N = 1,051).
- **Factor analyses** support the construct structure of the clinical subscales.

## Sample Items (from published review literature)

1. "My mood can shift quite suddenly." *(Borderline Features — Affective Instability)*
2. "I sometimes feel I have a special relationship with God." *(Mania — Grandiosity)*
3. "Sometimes I feel I can't control my temper." *(Aggression)*

*(These items appear in published PAI overview chapters and review papers.)*

## Chinese Version Status

- The PAI has been translated into multiple languages (Spanish, French, German, etc.), but a **formally published and normed Chinese (Mandarin) edition** with comprehensive psychometric validation is **not widely documented** in the English-language literature as of 2025.
- Some Chinese-language research studies have used translated versions in mainland China, Taiwan, and Hong Kong, but these are typically researcher-translated versions without official PAR authorization/norms.
- The **Chinese Personality Assessment Inventory (CPAI)** is a distinct instrument developed specifically for Chinese populations and is not a translation of the PAI.
