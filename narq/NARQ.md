## Construct

The NARQ is based on the **Narcissistic Admiration and Rivalry Concept (NARC)**, a two-dimensional process model of grandiose narcissism. It distinguishes between:

- **Admiration** (agentic/bright side): Self-enhancement through grandiose fantasies, striving for uniqueness, and charming behavior.
- **Rivalry** (antagonistic/dark side): Self-protection through devaluation of others, striving for supremacy, and aggressive behavior.

Each dimension comprises three facets reflecting affective-motivational, cognitive, and behavioral processes:

| Dimension   | Facet (Process)           | Items   |
|-------------|---------------------------|---------|
| Admiration  | Grandiosity (Cognitive)   | 1, 2, 3 |
| Admiration  | Uniqueness (Affective-Motivational) | 4, 5, 6 |
| Admiration  | Charmingness (Behavioral) | 7, 8, 9 |
| Rivalry     | Devaluation (Cognitive)   | 10, 11, 12 |
| Rivalry     | Supremacy (Affective)     | 13, 14, 15 |
| Rivalry     | Aggressiveness (Behavioral) | 16, 17, 18 |

## Items -- NARQ Full Form (18 Items)

> **Note:** Items below are grouped by facet for readability. The published NARQ interleaves Admiration and Rivalry items (official numbering: Admiration = 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 15, 16, 18; Rivalry = 4, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 17). For administration, use the official interleaved order from https://www.persoc.net.

### Admiration

#### Grandiosity (Affective-Motivational)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 1 | I am great. |
| 2 | I will someday be famous. |
| 3 | I deserve to be seen as a great personality. |

#### Uniqueness (Cognitive)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 4 | I show others how special I am. |
| 5 | I enjoy my successes very much. |
| 6 | Being a very special person gives me a lot of strength. |

#### Charmingness (Behavioral)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 7 | Most of the time I am able to draw people's attention to myself in conversations. |
| 8 | I manage to be the centre of attention with my outstanding contributions. |
| 9 | Mostly, I am very adept at dealing with other people. |

### Rivalry

#### Devaluation (Cognitive)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 10 | Most people won't achieve anything. |
| 11 | Other people are worth nothing. |
| 12 | Most people are somehow losers. |

#### Supremacy (Affective-Motivational)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 13 | I secretly take pleasure in the failure of my rivals. |
| 14 | I want my rivals to fail. |
| 15 | I enjoy it when another person is inferior to me. |

#### Aggressiveness (Behavioral)

| # | Item |
|---|------|
| 16 | I react annoyed if another person steals the show from me. |
| 17 | I often get annoyed when I am criticized. |
| 18 | I can barely stand it if another person is at the centre of events. |

## NARQ-S Short Form (6 Items)

The NARQ-S (Leckelt et al., 2016) consists of 6 items, one from each facet. Items use the same 1--6 response scale.

| # | Dimension | Facet | Item | Official NARQ # |
|---|-----------|-------|------|:--------------:|
| 1 | Admiration | Uniqueness | Being a very special person gives me a lot of strength. | 15 |
| 2 | Admiration | Charmingness | I manage to be the centre of attention with my outstanding contributions. | 16 |
| 3 | Admiration | Grandiosity | I deserve to be seen as a great personality. | 8 |
| 4 | Rivalry | Aggressiveness | I react annoyed if another person steals the show from me. | 4 |
| 5 | Rivalry | Supremacy | I want my rivals to fail. | 9 |
| 6 | Rivalry | Devaluation | Most people are somehow losers. | 17 |

### NARQ-S Scoring

- **Admiration** = Mean of NARQ-S items 1, 2, 3
- **Rivalry** = Mean of NARQ-S items 4, 5, 6

---

## Reverse Scoring

No items are reverse-scored.

---

## Scoring

1. **Admiration** = Mean of items 1--9 (range 1--6)
2. **Rivalry** = Mean of items 10--18 (range 1--6)
3. **Total Narcissism** = Mean of all 18 items (range 1--6; optional, as the two-dimensional model is preferred)

Facet-level scores can also be computed as the mean of each 3-item facet.

## Psychometric Properties

### Internal Consistency (Cronbach's alpha)

| Scale | Back et al. (2013) | Chinese Version (Deng et al., 2017) |
|-------|:------------------:|:-----------------------------------:|
| Admiration | .81--.87 | .80--.86 (range across subscales) |
| Rivalry | .78--.85 | .80--.86 (range across subscales) |

### Factor Structure

- The two-factor model (Admiration vs. Rivalry) with six first-order facets shows good fit in CFA.
- Chinese version CFA: The original two-factor structure did not show ideal fit in the Chinese adolescent sample (chi-sq = 965.71, df = 134, CFI = .82, TLI = .79, RMSEA = .13), suggesting possible cultural or age-related differences. A competitive narcissism model showed better fit.

### Convergent/Discriminant Validity

- **Admiration** correlates positively with self-esteem, extraversion, agentic communion, and social potency.
- **Rivalry** correlates positively with psychopathy, Machiavellianism, aggression, and antisocial behavior.
- **Admiration** correlates weakly or not at all with measures of antagonism; **Rivalry** correlates weakly with measures of agency.
- Both dimensions correlate positively with the Narcissistic Personality Inventory (NPI) total score.

### Test-Retest Reliability

Reported as adequate across multiple studies (specific coefficients vary by sample).
