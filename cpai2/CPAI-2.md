## Key Feature: Interpersonal Relatedness (IR) Factor

The CPAI-2's most distinctive contribution is the **Interpersonal Relatedness** factor, which captures culture-specific personality constructs not represented in the Western Big Five model:

- **Ren Qing**: Adherence to cultural norms of social reciprocity, courtesy rituals, exchanging resources, maintaining relational ties, and nepotism.
- **Harmony**: Emphasis on interpersonal peacefulness and avoidance of conflict.
- **Traditionalism vs. Modernity**: Attitudes toward traditional cultural beliefs (filial piety, family rituals, social conventions).
- **Face**: Concern with social reputation and public image.
- **Thrift vs. Extravagance**: Careful spending, saving, and frugality norms.

Joint factor analyses of CPAI-2 and NEO-PI-R in Chinese samples yield four shared factors (similar to N, E, A, C) plus Interpersonal Relatedness and Openness as distinct factors.

## Scoring

- Raw scores per scale converted to T-scores (M = 50, SD = 10) using sex-specific norms.
- Separate norms for Hong Kong, mainland China, and other populations.
- Profile interpretation based on elevation patterns across personality and clinical scales.

---

## Psychometric Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Internal Consistency (alpha, personality scales) | .55 -- .83 (median ~ .70) |
| Internal Consistency (alpha, clinical scales) | .55 -- .82 |
| Test-Retest (4 weeks) | .63 -- .87 |
| Normative Samples | Hong Kong (~2,000), mainland China (~2,000+) |

- Factor structure replicated across Hong Kong and mainland Chinese samples.
- The four-factor structure has been validated in cross-cultural samples (e.g., Singapore, South Korea, the U.S.).
- Interpersonal Relatedness factor shown to add incremental validity beyond Big Five for predicting culturally relevant criteria.
- English version available (Cheung, Cheung, Leung, Ward, & Leong, 2003), validated with multiethnic samples.

## Sample Items

Exact items are copyrighted. The CPAI-2 uses True/False statements covering daily life situations, values, interpersonal behaviors, and emotional reactions. Illustrative topics from published descriptions include:

1. Items measuring **Ren Qing** concern reciprocal social obligations (e.g., gift-giving customs, maintaining relational networks).
2. Items measuring **Harmony** address preferences for avoiding conflict and maintaining peaceful relationships.
3. Items measuring **Thrift** concern attitudes toward spending and saving money.

> Note: Actual item content is copyrighted by CUHK. Topic descriptions are paraphrased from published review articles.

## Chinese Version Status

The CPAI-2 was **originally developed in Chinese** (both Traditional and Simplified character versions). It is a native Chinese instrument, not a translation from English:

- **Languages**: Chinese (Traditional -- Hong Kong; Simplified -- mainland China), English, Korean
- **Primary norms**: Chinese populations (Hong Kong, mainland China)
- **English version**: Available for cross-cultural research (Cheung et al., 2003)
- **Adolescent version**: CPAI-A (adolescent form) available separately
