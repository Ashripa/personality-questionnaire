# IIP-64 / IIP-32 — Inventory of Interpersonal Problems

## APA Citation

Horowitz, L. M., Alden, L. E., Wiggins, J. S., & Pincus, A. L. (2000). *Inventory of Interpersonal Problems manual*. San Antonio, TX: The Psychological Corporation.

Alden, L. E., Wiggins, J. S., & Pincus, A. L. (1990). Construction of circumplex scales for the Inventory of Interpersonal Problems. *Journal of Personality Assessment, 55*(3-4), 521–536.

---

## Overview

The IIP is a self-report measure of interpersonal difficulties organized around the **interpersonal circumplex** model, with two orthogonal dimensions: **Dominance (Agency)** and **Affiliation (Communion)**. Two versions exist:

| Version | Items | Items per subscale | Administration time |
|---------|-------|--------------------|---------------------|
| IIP-64  | 64    | 8                  | 15–20 min           |
| IIP-32  | 32    | 4                  | 10 min              |

---

## Scale Structure (8 Octant Subscales)

Both versions share the same eight circumplex subscales; only item count per subscale differs.

| Octant Code | Subscale Name        | IIP-64 Items | IIP-32 Items | Circumplex Location |
|-------------|----------------------|:------------:|:------------:|---------------------|
| PA          | Domineering/Controlling | 8          | 4            | High Dom, Neutral Aff |
| BC          | Vindictive/Self-Centered | 8         | 4            | High Dom, Low Aff   |
| DE          | Cold/Distant         | 8            | 4            | Neutral Dom, Low Aff |
| FG          | Socially Inhibited   | 8            | 4            | Low Dom, Low Aff    |
| HI          | Nonassertive         | 8            | 4            | Low Dom, Neutral Aff |
| JK          | Overly Accommodating | 8            | 4            | Low Dom, High Aff   |
| LM          | Self-Sacrificing     | 8            | 4            | Neutral Dom, High Aff |
| NO          | Intrusive/Needy      | 8            | 4            | High Dom, High Aff  |

### Item Format

- **Part 1 (behavioral inhibitions):** Stem = "It is hard for me to..." (e.g., say "no" to others, be assertive)
- **Part 2 (behavioral excesses):** Stem = "...I do too much" / "These are things I do too much" (e.g., argue, open up to people)

### Scoring

- **Circumplex indices:** Computed from octant scores — angular displacement, amplitude (overall interpersonal distress), and elevation (general distress).

---

## Response Format

5-point Likert scale: 0 = *Not at all* to 4 = *Extremely*

---

## Administration

- **Mode:** Paper-and-pencil or computerized
- **Population:** Adults (18+); clinical and non-clinical
- **Reading level:** Approximately 6th grade
- **IIP-32** was developed as a briefer screening version for clinical settings

---

## Psychometric Properties

### IIP-64
- **Internal consistency:** Cronbach's alpha = .72–.85 across subscales (Horowitz et al., 2000)
- **Test-retest reliability:** r = .78–.98 (10-week interval)
- **Factor structure:** Two-factor circumplex structure (Dominance and Affiliation) confirmed via PCA
- **Convergent validity:** Correlates with Big Five (e.g., Cold/DE with low Agreeableness), SCL-90-R, and BDI

### IIP-32
- **Internal consistency:** Generally somewhat lower than IIP-64 (alpha = .68–.82)
- **Factor structure:** Circumplex structure replicated but fit indices tend to be weaker
- **Primary advantage:** Feasibility as screening tool in time-limited clinical settings

---

## Sample Items (from published literature)

1. "It is hard for me to **say 'no' to other people**." (HI — Nonassertive)
2. "It is hard for me to **show affection to people**." (DE — Cold/Distant)
3. "I am **too aggressive toward other people**." (PA — Domineering)

*(These items have appeared in published validation articles and review chapters.)*

## Chinese Version Status

- A **Chinese translation of the IIP-SC (Short Circumplex)** has been validated (Shen et al., 2014, published in *Assessment*). The study provided preliminary validity evidence supporting use with Chinese-speaking populations, though some subscale reliability was modest.
- Full IIP-64 Chinese adaptations have been used in research samples in mainland China and Taiwan, but a formally published, commercially normed Chinese edition with comprehensive psychometric documentation is **not yet widely available** as of 2025.
- Reference: Shen, Y., et al. (2014). A Chinese translation of the Inventory of Interpersonal Problems-Short Circumplex. *PubMed PMID: 25365777*.
