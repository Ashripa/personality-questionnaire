## Scoring

- Reverse-score items 11, 15, 17, 20, 25 (6 − raw)
- **Mean** of each 9-item subscale (range: 1.00–5.00)
- No composite "Dark Triad" total in original; subscales interpreted independently
- Higher scores = stronger dark trait

### Psychometric Properties

- Internal consistency: α = .71–.77 per subscale (Jones & Paulhus, 2014)
- 中文版 (SD3-C): Zhang et al. (2020) confirmed cross-cultural factor congruence (N=950)
